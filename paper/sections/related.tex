\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Nibli combines established mechanisms. The relevant comparison is therefore
which contracts are integrated and measured, not whether symbolic inference,
controlled syntax, provenance, or premise updates first appear here.
Table~\ref{tab:related} summarizes the precedents closest to the research questions.
Its entries describe published capabilities, not outcomes of a new comparative
usability or feature-completeness study.

Attempto Controlled English (ACE) gives a precisely constrained English surface
and translates accepted text to discourse representation structures and other
formal targets. Its surrounding tools include paraphrasing, reasoning, and rule
execution~\cite{ace}. Nibli's predicate-call syntax pursues a more explicit
argument-place discipline. That difference can be described structurally, but
the present experiments do not establish that one surface is easier to author
or less likely to be misunderstood. A formal parse can be determinate while a
person still gives it an unintended reading.

Tabled logic programming supplies a long-established account of reusing subgoals,
resuming suspended computations, and establishing completion. XSB also supports
negation and incremental treatment of changing programs~\cite{xsb}.
Nibli's depth-sensitive caches should not be equated with a complete SLG
evaluation strategy. Nor is combining top-down and bottom-up computation itself
new: Warren describes their procedural integration through tabled
evaluation~\cite{topdown-bottomup}. The contribution here is the measured behavior
of Nibli's particular completion requests, bounded outcomes, state reconstruction,
and evidence interface.

\Souffle\ synthesizes executable analyzers from Datalog~\cite{souffle}.
Its provenance work augments evaluation with proof annotations and supports
construction of partial proof trees for debugging~\cite{souffle-provenance}.
It would therefore be incorrect to motivate Nibli by claiming that Datalog
systems do not explain their answers. Our compiled \Souffle\ baseline measures
ordinary evaluation with provenance disabled; it establishes no relative
provenance overhead or certificate-strength result. Nibli's envelope binds
profile, outcome, and trace, while the structural checker remains weaker than
an independent semantic proof verifier.

The Backward/Forward maintenance algorithm studied with RDFox seeks alternative
derivations when facts are deleted and compares that work with
Delete/Rederive~\cite{rdfox-bf}. This is directly relevant to the sole-support
and alternative-support cases. Nibli instead reconstructs from retained
assertions on withdrawal. Those designs address related correctness concerns
with different update mechanisms. The new experiments do not measure RDFox
updates or support a claim that reconstruction is generally faster.

Cedar's verification-guided development combines proved executable models with
differential random testing against production code and property-based testing
of unmodeled components~\cite{cedar}. It is a methodological precedent for
Nibli's mixed assurance argument, not a baseline theorem prover. In both cases,
the distinction between a proved model and evidence connecting production
implementation to that model matters.

Logic-LM uses a language model to formulate a problem, a symbolic solver to
reason over the formulation, and solver feedback for refinement~\cite{logic-lm}.
This separates the quality of formalization from the reliability of solving an
accepted formulation. Nibli has an optional formalization workflow, but no
language model is in the measured reasoning loop. We make no comparative
language-model accuracy claim, and solver success alone does not validate an
intended natural-language meaning.
