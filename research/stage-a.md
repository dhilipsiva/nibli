## A.3 §1 Product boundary

### Primary identity: a compiler `[proposal]`

The product's primary identity is a **compiler** — from a typed declarative scientific surface language to (a) a validated canonical model, (b) typed solver obligations, and (c) an auditable receipt binding the two. Everything distinctive about the product happens at compile time: fail-closed name/unit/identity/context resolution, dimensional and stoichiometric validation, validity-regime checking, obligation planning, and the epistemology of §A.5. The other candidate identities are settled as follows:

- **Orchestrator** — secondary. It is the runtime of the compiler's output, and §A.8's workflow-engine survey shows the undifferentiated parts (containerised invocation, caching, resume, scheduling) are commodity. The product's orchestration value is *typed obligations and typed results*, not job management.
- **Theorem prover** — absent. The product *consumes* proof objects and certificates; it does not originate mathematics. (Non-goal below.)
- **Notebook / UI** — absent from v1. The compiler is a library + CLI; interactive frontends come later and are not architectural.
- **Workflow engine** — absent. Where multi-step pipelines exceed the orchestrator's job model, the product should *emit* an existing workflow format rather than grow one (Stage D decides which, using §A.8 evidence).
- **Reproducibility system** — partially constitutive: receipts are a core output, but the product does not manage environments; it *records* them (and can lean on Nix, per §A.8) `[proposal]`.
- **Modeling language** — constitutive but instrumental: the language exists to feed the compiler; language design (Stage B/C) follows semantics.

### First-party Rust core vs. external engines `[proposal]`

**In the Rust core** (first-party, in the soundness TCB): surface parser; schema/ontology resolver; type, shape, unit and dimension checker; chemical-identity resolver (normative perception model chosen in Stage B §6); context and validity-regime checker; canonicalizer; exact arithmetic (rationals; intervals for enclosures) sufficient for stoichiometric linear algebra and unit conversion; first-order uncertainty-propagation algebra with declared correlations; the logical inference kernel (whether that is Nibli is exactly Stage C's question — nothing in Stage A assumes it); obligation planner; receipt generator; evidence store.

**External engines** (typed backends behind the Stage D protocol, never described as Rust components): computer algebra beyond exact linear algebra; ODE/PDE and stochastic simulation; optimization; quantum chemistry; sequence/variant bioinformatics; statistical inference beyond the built-in consistency criterion. License consequence from §A.1(1): GPL-family engines integrate at process or service boundaries only.

### Operations that must fail closed (per the §0.5 contract)

Name resolution (undeclared predicate, function, entity, or unit is a compile error, never a warning); unit and dimension checking (no default unit, ever); chemical identity resolution (ambiguity between perception models or tautomer forms is an error naming the ambiguity, not a silent pick); mandatory-context validation (a gas volume without T and P and a dry/saturated declaration, a genomic coordinate without assembly + base convention + strand, a measurement without uncertainty — all compile errors); closure and vocabulary declarations (late or unauthorized closure refused); solver selection (no silent default solver, precision, or approximation — an unconstrained choice point is an error asking for a policy). The observable contract is §0.5's: no result artifact on rejection; span + machine-readable code + the specific offending entity; exit status distinguishing rejected-input / internal-error / backend-unavailable; no silent substitution. Which of the seventeen §8 compiler stages can honour this is Stage C's per-stage table.

### Explicit non-goals for the first release

1. **Not a theorem prover.** Kernel-checked mathematics is imported evidence (§A.5 categories; Stage D trust axes), never a first-party capability.
2. **Not a notebook, IDE, or visualization tool.**
3. **Not a workflow engine** — emit or invoke one; never grow one.
4. **Not a data lake.** Large artifacts (sequences, trajectories, images) are external typed references with hashes; the threshold and mechanism are Stage B §7 work.
5. **Not an ontology author.** The product adopts identifier namespaces (CURIEs into ChEBI, GO, NCBI Taxonomy, …) and pins versions; it does not mint competing term hierarchies. (Local schemas for *model* vocabulary are the product's own; that is the §A.1(5) governance risk.)
6. **Not an LLM science oracle.** LLMs are authoring-side only (§A.5.9); no natural-language query interface in v1.
7. **Not a distributed scheduler.** v1 orchestration is local: in-process + subprocess jobs with cancellation; cluster/cloud execution arrives, if ever, by emitting workflow formats.
8. **Not an availability-engineered service.** Availability TCB explicitly out of scope for v1 per §0.5, consistent with the §A.1(3) latency model — a hosted service raises this later, and that is recorded as a deferred, not ignored, decision.
9. **Domain narrowing per §A.9** — the implemented v1 scope is *biochemical reaction networks: chemistry + biology co-resident*, with physics-of-measurement (units, gas-law-class model laws, CODATA constants) and mathematics (exact arithmetic, uncertainty algebra) present as infrastructure, not as served domains. Details and the pair argument in §A.9.

### What each result status claims — and does not claim

Stated once here, formalized in §A.4: a `proved` claims derivability from the declared axioms and closures of the assembled context — nothing empirical. A `supported`/`refuted` claims consistency/inconsistency between declared model and cited evidence **under a named statistical criterion** — never truth or falsity of the model in the world, and never generalization beyond the declared validity regime. `conflicting` claims only that cited sources disagree and no declared policy resolves them. `unknown` claims the question is well-formed, in scope, and unsettled — a stable epistemic state, not an apology. `out_of_regime` claims the system *knows why it cannot answer*: every applicable model's declared validity regime excludes the inputs. No status ever claims empirical confirmation from internal consistency (§0.5 "auditable" caveat: provenance is not validity).

*Confidence: medium-high — the identity choice and core/external split are proposals argued from the survey and inputs, not yet validated by Stage B/C detail. Largest unverified assumption: that exact stoichiometric linear algebra and first-order correlated uncertainty propagation are small enough to be first-party Rust within the team budget — plausibility rests on their being textbook algorithms (rational Gaussian elimination / Hermite normal form; covariance-matrix push-through), but no sizing spike has been run and none is permitted until Stage C.*

## A.4 §1 Result taxonomy — two axes, formalized

### Structure `[proposal]`

```rust
// Provisional sketch — labels normative, shapes illustrative (per prompt rule on pseudocode).
enum Outcome {
    Verdict {
        verdict: Verdict,          // axis 1 — what is claimed about the proposition
        basis: BasisRef,           // context snapshot + evidence set + criterion + closures consulted
        warnings: Vec<Warning>,    // non-fatal disclosures (approximation applied, tolerance stretched)
    },
    NoVerdict {
        disposition: Disposition,  // axis 2 — why no verdict was produced
        detail: DispositionDetail, // mandatory typed payload, per-variant (see table)
    },
}
```

The axes are exclusive in the result — an evaluation yields a verdict **or** a disposition, never both — but every `Verdict` carries a `basis` and every `NoVerdict` carries a mandatory payload, so neither axis is ever a bare enum tag. `unknown` is a **verdict** (an epistemic state about the proposition), not a disposition: the system examined everything declared and the question is genuinely unsettled. Every disposition, by contrast, means the examination itself did not complete, and names what would unblock it.

### Axis 1 — verdicts

| Verdict | Claims | Does **not** claim |
|---|---|---|
| `proved` | A checkable proof object derives the proposition from the declared axioms, facts, and closures of the assembled context. | Anything about the world. A proof under a wrong model is a correct proof of a wrong model's consequence. |
| `disproved` | The negation is `proved`. **Logical.** | Empirical falsity. |
| `supported` | Cited evidence is consistent with the proposition under a stated model and a named statistical criterion. | Truth, confirmation, or that no other model fits better. |
| `refuted` | Cited evidence is inconsistent with the proposition under a stated model and a named statistical criterion. **Empirical, and model-relative.** | Logical impossibility, or falsity outside the declared regime, organism, or conditions. |
| `conflicting` | Sourced claims disagree; no declared resolution policy applies. | That either side is wrong. |
| `unknown` | Well-formed, in scope, unsettled by declared evidence and computation. | That the answer is unknowable, or that more evidence/computation could not settle it. |
| `out_of_regime` | Well-formed, but the inputs fall outside the declared validity regime of every applicable model — the system knows *why* it cannot answer. | That no model could ever apply; only that none *declared* does. |

**The `disproved` / `refuted` distinction, stated explicitly as the prompt requires:** `disproved` is a fact about a formal system — the negation is derivable, and would remain so on a planet with different physics, because it appeals only to the declared axioms. `refuted` is a fact about the relationship between a model and measurements — it is empirical, criterion-dependent, uncertainty-dependent, and regime-scoped, and it can be undone by better measurements, a different declared criterion, or the discovery that the regime declaration was violated (the §14 Crabtree case is exactly this: `refuted` for *complete aerobic respiration under these conditions*, with a regime warning — never "respiration is false"). Collapsing these two is how a stoichiometry checker gets quoted as having "disproven" a biological hypothesis. `[proposal]` (the distinction is the prompt's; the planet phrasing and the consequence are this report's).

**`out_of_regime` vs `unknown`:** `unknown` invites more evidence; `out_of_regime` invites a different *model* — or a regime-extension. They demand different user actions, which is the test for keeping enum variants apart. `[inference]`

### Axis 2 — dispositions, with mandatory payloads

| Disposition | Mandatory payload | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `invalid_input` | source span, machine-readable code, offending entity | Rejected at compile time; the fail-closed §0.5 contract governs. |
| `invalid_model` | the violated validation (unbalanced reaction with the element imbalance; the inconsistent dimension equation; the unsatisfiable constraint core) | The model, not the query, failed. |
| `unsupported` | the named missing capability, and whether it is *planned* or *out of scope* (the §A.7 matrix's own levels) | Representable but not evaluable here. |
| `resource_exhausted` | which declared budget (time, memory, fuel, iterations) and its value | Retryable with a bigger budget — which `unknown` is not. |
| `backend_unavailable` | solver identity + version constraint that could not be satisfied | Never silently degraded to another solver. |
| `non_convergent` | residual, iteration count, method identity | A method outcome, not a negative result. |
| `indeterminate` | the alternatives that cannot be distinguished, the margin, and the **source** of the indeterminacy: `numerical` (method resolution) or `measurement` (propagated input uncertainty) | See amendment below. |

**Amendment to the prompt's taxonomy `[proposal]`:** the prompt's `numerically_indeterminate` ("converged, but the result is not distinguishable from an alternative within the error model") is generalized here to `indeterminate` with a mandatory *source* field. Reason: the prompt's own vertical slice demands exactly this outcome for the limiting-reagent question — "the determination is not resolved at the stated uncertainty, with the margin reported" — but there the indistinguishability comes from *propagated measurement uncertainty*, not from numerical error. One disposition with a typed source keeps the two honest instead of forcing the measurement case to masquerade as a numerical one (or worse, as `unknown`, which would lose the margin payload). The prompt's variant name survives as `indeterminate{source: numerical}`.

**Amendments adopted after prior-art and red-team review `[proposal]`** (motivations: A.8.17, A.10 W13):

1. **Three added dispositions:** `cancelled` (who cancelled, at which stage — required once §10 decides obligations are cancellable, which the §A.1(3) latency model implies); `policy_refused` (the solver was reachable but the declared trust policy forbade it — distinct from `backend_unavailable`, which must never be silently degraded into); `gave_up` (the procedure is incomplete and honestly stopped without exhausting a budget — SZS's `GaveUp`/`Incomplete`, which two decades of CASC practice show is a real, distinct outcome).
2. **Enclosure-established claims are `proved`, with the certificate kind recorded.** A §0.5 level-2 result (rigorous enclosure) that certifies a claim is a proof-strength artifact: it is classified `proved`, and the *basis* records certificate kind = enclosure/subdivision-replay, with Stage D's verification-strength axis distinguishing kernel-checked proof from independently checkable certificate. This closes the red-team's "unclassifiable enclosure" gap without inventing a third verdict.
3. **`invalid_model` stays a disposition for v1, flagged `[open]`.** SZS classifies `ContradictoryAxioms` as a *success* — a semantic determination about the input — which is a respectable argument that provable model inconsistency belongs on the verdict axis. v1 keeps it pre-evaluation (fail-closed at assembly, A.5.2); the question is registered for Stage B rather than silently settled.
4. **The taxonomy is an extension of SZS, and the mapping is part of the design:** Theorem→`proved`, CounterSatisfiable→`disproved`, Unknown→`unknown`, Timeout/MemoryOut/ResourceOut→`resource_exhausted`, Error→`invalid_input`, Inappropriate→`unsupported`, GaveUp/Incomplete→`gave_up`; the empirical verdicts (`supported`/`refuted`/`conflicting`/`out_of_regime`) and the numerical dispositions (`non_convergent`/`indeterminate`) are exactly the two families the logic ecosystem never needed — the product's additions, not reinventions.

Prompt's non-conflation rules, restated as invariants the taxonomy now enforces structurally: absence ≠ negation (`unknown` ≠ `disproved`); solver failure ≠ negative result (`non_convergent`/`backend_unavailable` are dispositions, unreachable from Axis 1); unsupported ≠ unknown (different axes entirely); model inconsistency ≠ empirical falsification (`invalid_model` disposition vs `refuted` verdict — one is pre-evaluation, one is an evaluation's outcome).

*Confidence: high for the structure (it is the prompt's own two-axis design, formalized); medium for the `indeterminate` amendment, which is this report's proposal and should be attacked in Stage B when uncertainty semantics are defined. Largest unverified assumption: that no evaluation ever needs to return a verdict **and** a disposition simultaneously — the design forces disposition-like partialities into `warnings`, and a case that genuinely straddles (e.g. a `supported` verdict obtained at a stretched tolerance) is currently a warning-bearing verdict, which Stage D's receipt schema must make impossible to overlook.*

## A.5 §2 Scientific epistemology and evidence

### A.5.1 The claim-kind system

The seventeen §2 categories (definition, axiom, theorem, hypothesis, model law, assumption, approximation, observation, measurement, imported database assertion, dataset, logically derived result, exact computed result, approximate computed result, simulation result, statistical inference, expert annotation, external citation) are **structurally distinct claim kinds** in one evidence graph: each kind fixes a different set of *mandatory* fields, so the distinctions are enforced by the type system rather than by discipline `[proposal]`. Illustrative contrasts: a `measurement` requires an uncertainty, a protocol/instrument reference, and a context record; an `axiom` requires a theory scope and has no uncertainty; an `approximation` requires the quantity it approximates and a validity condition; an `imported database assertion` requires source, version, license, and retrieval date; a `simulation result` requires the model version, solver identity, and seed. A claim missing a kind-mandatory field does not enter the graph — fail closed, authoring-side.

### A.5.2 Conflict, contexts, and the paraconsistency decision

Conflicting sourced claims **coexist** in the evidence graph as distinct, attributed, versioned claims — the graph is a record of *who asserted what*, and records of disagreement are not contradictions in a logic. Inference never runs over the raw graph. A query runs in an assembled **context**: a named, versioned snapshot that imports specific claims, models, closures, and policies. Within an assembled context the logic is classical and the assembly step *checks consistency* of what it can (dimensional, stoichiometric, direct contradiction among imported ground claims); an inconsistent assembly is `invalid_model`, fail closed, before any query runs. Contradictions *across* contexts are unremarkable — that is what contexts are for.

**Paraconsistency decision `[proposal]`: no paraconsistent logic anywhere.** The need it would serve is met structurally: the evidence graph is contradiction-tolerant because it is a database of attributed claims, not a theory; the reasoning context is contradiction-free because assembly fails closed. The boundary of the "paraconsistent region" is therefore the context-assembly line, and it is sharp. If two sources disagree and both must inform one query, the query either runs under a declared resolution policy (next paragraph) or returns `conflicting` with both claims cited. This buys the practical benefit (local contradictions don't poison the system) without the cost (paraconsistent consequence relations are unfamiliar to users and would make every proof object harder to check).

**Resolution policies are declared artifacts**, not defaults: `prefer_newer`, `prefer_source_rank(ordering)`, `manual(claim_id)` — each use is recorded in the receipt's basis. No policy declared ⇒ `conflicting`. A policy is itself versioned content: changing it invalidates caches like any semantic input (Stage D).

### A.5.3 Versioning

Claims are immutable. Change is supersession: a new claim version with an edge to its predecessor, never mutation. A claim's identity includes the schema version it was authored under; schema migration is an explicit, recorded transformation producing new claim versions, and a context pins claim versions, so "the same query" against a moved world is *visibly* a different context. `[proposal]`

### A.5.4 Model applicability — validity regimes as checked objects

A model declares its validity regime as **typed predicates over context variables** (temperature range, pressure range, concentration regime, organism/strain, phase, …), not prose. Regime checking is a compile/assembly-time operation with three outcomes: inside (proceed), outside (`out_of_regime`), and *undeterminable* — the context record lacks the variable the regime constrains, which is a fail-closed `invalid_input` naming the missing variable (this is what makes "missing water-vapour declaration" in §14 an error rather than a silent ideal-gas guess). The Crabtree centrepiece depends on this machinery: the declared respiration model can carry `glucose_concentration: low` as a regime predicate, and the verdict apparatus can then say `refuted` *and* warn that the regime may not have been met. `[proposal]`

### A.5.5 Context records

Each claim kind's mandatory context schema names the variables that must be present (organism, strain, tissue, temperature, pressure, pH, buffer, reference frame, coordinate system, time, protocol, …) — *which* variables are mandatory is domain-schema content (Stage B §5–§7), but the *mechanism* — missing mandatory context is a compile error, and context variables are typed and unit-checked like everything else — is common-layer (Stage B §3). Genomic coordinates are the canonical example the prompt supplies: assembly + coordinate base + strand are components of the coordinate *type*, not annotations `[proposal, adopting §7]`.

### A.5.6 Closure — explicit, scoped, owned

Open world is the default for every empirical relation. Closure is opt-in, per relation, per context: `closed(relation, context)` declares "this relation's extension in this context is complete." Two rules make it safe `[proposal]`:

1. **Authority:** only the context owner can close a relation in that context — the model author for model-local relations, the dataset publisher (as a signed part of the dataset's manifest) for dataset-complete relations. A closure declaration is itself a claim, with provenance, subject to review like any claim. Nobody can close a relation in a context they merely import.
2. **Visibility (the wrong-closure defence):** any verdict that *used* a closure — any FALSE-by-absence, any universally-quantified TRUE over a closed extension — lists that closure in its basis, and rendered output must distinguish "FALSE because derivably false" from "FALSE because absent from a relation that `<owner>` declared complete." A wrong closure then produces not a silent false negative but a *citable* one: the receipt names the declaration to blame. This is the authoring-integrity analogue of Nibli's disclosed closed-world contract ("FALSE means not derivable") `[local-fact: README/GUARANTEES framing per CLAUDE.md]`, upgraded with attribution. Detection beyond visibility (e.g. statistical canaries for suspiciously productive closures) is deferred — `[open]`, revisited with Stage D's authoring-side threat row.

Ordering is enforced, not documented: a closure arriving after queries already ran in that context version is a new context version — there is no in-place tightening that silently re-reads old answers. `[proposal, pattern lifted from Nibli's admits-ordering rule [local-fact: CLAUDE.md pins/closed-vocabulary-order]]`

### A.5.7 The seven separated uncertainties

Kept as **distinct typed fields with no common scale and no combination operator except explicit, named ones**: logical truth status (a verdict, never a number); measurement uncertainty (a distribution or interval attached to a measurement claim, with the declared correlation structure — §14's shared-T,P case makes correlation first-class, not an afterthought); numerical error (attached to computed results: the §0.5 five-level exactness class, plus bound or estimate where levels 2–3 apply); stochastic variability (seed-indexed spread of stochastic simulation outputs); parameter uncertainty (attached to model parameters, distinct from measurement uncertainty of inputs); structural/model uncertainty (never a number in v1 — represented only as the existence of alternative models and `refuted`/`supported` verdicts per model; any attempt to quantify it is out of scope `[proposal]`); source/evidence quality (an attribute of provenance — Stage D's second trust axis — never multiplied into any of the others). The banned operation is the one the prompt bans: a single collapsed confidence score.

### A.5.9 LLM scope decision (required by §2)

**LLMs are in scope, authoring-side only, behind deterministic gates `[proposal]`:**

- **In scope:** English→surface-language formalization assistance; schema/mapping suggestion; literature-extraction *drafts*. Every LLM output must pass the deterministic compiler (fail-closed, same as hand-written input) before it exists as a claim — the pattern Nibli already ships as `nibli-formalize` (LLM proposal → parse/compile/round-trip gates → fresh-context semantic verification) `[local-fact: CLAUDE.md nibli-formalize row]`, which is working evidence the pattern is buildable by this team.
- **Provenance:** any claim whose text originated from an LLM carries `extracted_by_nondeterministic_process` provenance (Stage D's second trust axis) and **cannot participate in a verdict** until promoted by a human reviewer or by a deterministic validator (e.g. an extracted melting point re-checked against the cited table). LLM output is never evidence; at best it is a *draft of a claim someone else must own*.
- **Out of scope for v1:** natural-language querying; LLM-mediated result explanation (rendering stays deterministic, from proof objects and receipts).

### A.5.10 Prior-art reinforcements, and the standing feasibility warning

Added after the survey returned; the sections above stand, with these strengthenings. (1) **Contexts have shipped prior art**: Cyc's microtheories (A.8.16) are the A.5.2 design in production form — assumption bundles as factored-out conjuncts, first-class contexts in a specialization hierarchy, explicit lifting rules — and supply a formalization this report adopts: **`out_of_regime` is a failed lift**, a query whose supporting claims cannot be soundly imported into the query's context; structural uncertainty *requires* contexts, because no type system lets contradictory axiom sets coexist. Cyc's correction is equally adopted: lifting must be explicit, fail-closed, and deterministic, never defeasible argumentation. `[proposal]` (2) **Measurement uncertainty binds to GUM**: each uncertainty component carries its JCGM Type A/B evaluation provenance, budgets are auditable decompositions, and JCGM 101 is the normative Monte Carlo propagation reference — adopting a live metrology standard instead of inventing vocabulary (A.8.14). `[proposal]` (3) **The aleatory/epistemic discipline is adopted from Dakota/V&V** where it applies: epistemic components as intervals/structures, never forced into distributions, mixed propagation yielding interval-valued results (A.8.15). `[proposal]`

**The standing warning (top feasibility risk of the epistemology):** the Stan survey's sharpest finding is that *no shipped system demonstrates the four-way uncertainty separation surviving inference* — a joint posterior mathematically fuses measurement, prior, and parameter uncertainty, and the folk theorem shows numerical and structural failure entangle empirically. Design consequence, registered now: the product maintains the separation **at the claim and propagation layer** (typed components with GUM-style budgets pushed through declared propagation operators), and v1 deliberately contains no joint Bayesian inference — the workflow where fusion is intrinsic. Stage B must exhibit the propagation algebra preserving per-component provenance end-to-end on the vertical slice, and failure to do so coherently is a pre-registered don't-build trigger (A.9). `[proposal]` `[open]`

*Confidence: medium-high — the mechanisms are proposals, but each has either a working precedent in the local repo (closure visibility, formalize-gates), a shipped external precedent found by the survey (microtheories, GUM, aleatory/epistemic typing), or a direct requirement in the prompt (regime checking, uncertainty separation); none contradicts survey evidence. Largest unverified assumption: that context-assembly consistency checking can be made cheap enough for the interactive latency budget — assembly-time checks are bounded by context size, not evidence-graph size, which is the design intent, but no sizing has been done and none is permitted until Stage C.*

## A.7 Operational definition of "comprehensive" + capability matrix

**"Comprehensive" means exactly:** every workflow in the §0.5 list has an explicit row; every row carries one of the seven coverage levels; every row at level *representable* or above traces to at least one competency question from §A.6; and no capability exists outside the matrix. Anything else — vocabulary size, plugin count — is not comprehensiveness. `[proposal]`

Coverage levels (the prompt's seven): **R** representable in the surface language · **V** statically validated · **N** natively evaluated · **D** delegated to a typed backend · **I** imported as externally trusted evidence · **P** planned but unsupported · **X** deliberately outside scope. A row may carry several (a reaction is R+V natively, its kinetics D).

| Workflow | v1 level | Bounded by | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Logical derivation | N | CQ-M1, CQ-P2 | finite, context-scoped; the Stage C kernel question |
| Theorem proving | X (import: I) | — | non-goal §A.3; kernel-checked proofs enter as evidence only |
| Exact symbolic computation | N (narrow) / P (general) | CQ-T2 | native: exact rational linear algebra for stoichiometry + unit algebra; general CAS is P |
| Approximate numerical computation | N (narrow) / D | CQ-A1, CQ-M1 | native: unit conversion, correlated first-order uncertainty propagation; heavier numerics D |
| Equation & constraint solving | V + N (linear, exact) / P (nonlinear) | CQ-T2 | balancing = exact linear algebra over ℚ |
| Optimization | P | — | no CQ requires it; planned |
| Deterministic simulation | P | — | the slice needs none; first D-target after v1 (SBML/ODE) |
| Stochastic simulation | X (v1) | — | revisit with simulation |
| Parameter estimation | X (v1) | — | |
| Statistical inference | N (minimal, declared criterion) / P (general) | CQ-M1, CQ-T1 | v1 ships exactly the consistency criteria `supported`/`refuted` need; Bayesian estimation is P |
| Empirical-data lookup | I | CQ-A1, CQ-M1 | versioned imports: CODATA, atomic weights, species data; each pinned + hashed |
| Annotation & ontology mapping | R + V | CQ-T1 | versioned CURIEs, checked; no reasoning over ontologies (§A.8 OWL lesson) |
| Model comparison | P | — | Crabtree follow-up ("which model fits?") is post-v1; v1 gives per-model verdicts |
| Consistency checking | N | CQ-T2, CQ-A2, CQ-T1 | dimensional, stoichiometric, identity, regime |
| Explanation & provenance | N | CQ-P1, CQ-P2 | receipts + provenance-DAG queries are core, not reporting |

Every P and X row is a *refusal the product can name* (`unsupported{planned}` / `unsupported{out_of_scope}` dispositions), which is what makes the matrix operational rather than aspirational. The matrix is v1-scoped by §A.9's narrowing; the surface language and IR must remain *representable* (R) beyond what v1 evaluates — that asymmetry (represent broadly, evaluate narrowly, refuse loudly) is the load-bearing design idea that lets the scope narrow without the language narrowing. `[proposal]`

*Confidence: medium — the matrix is coherent and bounded, but it is bounded by invented CQs (§A.6 caveat), and two rows (simulation, model comparison) are placed by the scope argument in §A.9 rather than by any user's stated need. Largest unverified assumption: that "represent broadly, evaluate narrowly" is actually achievable — that the surface language can honestly represent, say, a PDE-driven model while refusing to evaluate it, without either the representation degenerating into strings (the §0.5 fidelity failure) or the representable surface dragging v1 into implementing evaluation. Stage B owns this.*

## A.8 §0.6 Prior art

Method: seventeen system clusters surveyed in parallel by dedicated research agents against primary sources (official specs, maintainer docs, repositories, license texts), every version/license/adoption claim either verified against a source fetched on 2026-08-26 or tagged `[evidence-incomplete]`; a completeness critic then named four material omissions (Stan, Dakota/V&V-UQ, Cyc, TPTP/SZS — A.8.14–17), which were surveyed the same way. **The full per-system evidence — including the key-fact tables with URL + version + access date per claim — is archived under `research/stage-a-evidence/` (one file per cluster) and is part of this deliverable; the entries below are compressed digests.** Unless tagged otherwise, claims in this section are `[external-fact]` with the source recorded in the corresponding evidence file.

### A.8.1 Modelica / MSL / OpenModelica / Dymola / FMI

The closest existing thing to the physics core, and the answer to "is this Modelica plus a provenance layer" is **no — and the honest partial yes is "FMI-consuming orchestrator," not "extended Modelica."** Overlap is real on the simulation half: typed declarative acausal surface, fail-closed identifier resolution, a defined compile pipeline whose crown jewel (DAE index reduction: Pantelides/dummy-derivative, tearing) represents decades of engineering the product must never rebuild. But the gaps are structural: no entailment/verdict/proof-object machinery at all; **the unit system is fail-open by specification** — `unit` is a string attribute defaulting to `""` and MLS 3.7 treats unit-consistency checking as tool-dependent, its own text conceding a conforming tool may miss a conflict and "give the unexpected result of x being 5 meters" (specification.modelica.org/maint/3.7, accessed 2026-08-26); evidence/uncertainty have no language-level representation, and annotations — the only extension point — are legal to ignore, i.e. fail-open. Provenance is being retrofitted 25+ years in (SSP Traceability, ~2025) as documentation-grade metadata `[evidence-incomplete on the standard text — flagged; if it is machine-checked receipts the gap narrows]`. History lesson: the founding source-level-exchange ambition failed commercially; the minimal typed compiled-artifact seam — FMI, a small C API + typed XML — won 280+ tools against ~a dozen full-language implementations. **Adopt: FMI as one solver backend; the layered spec/library/implementation governance. Avoid: quality-of-implementation semantics — Modelica's costliest mistake and the direct negation of the product's thesis.**

### A.8.2 Julia ModelingToolkit / SciML

The closest math/physics *compiler*: symbolic-numeric acausal modeling, alias elimination/tearing/index reduction, codegen into the broadest open ODE-solver suite, plus Catalyst.jl as the domain-DSL precedent for reaction networks. Structurally absent: proof objects (transformations are uncertified rewrites), fail-closed vocabulary (`@variables` mints symbols ad hoc — a typo is a new unknown), per-result receipts (reproducibility is Manifest-level), typed uncertainty. Units are check-only and effectively optional: unannotated variables pass vacuously, the FAQ recommends the unitless path, and generated numerical code is unitless — units never constrain the solve. The governance warning is fresh: on 2025-12-06 the SciML steering council split MTK v11 — MIT `ModelingToolkitBase` vs **the advanced compiler passes moved behind AGPL under JuliaHub** — because large commercial users did not fund the permissive core (discourse.julialang.org/t/134396, accessed 2026-08-26). **Adopt: the compiler/solver split with typed problem objects; the v10 one-unified-system-type lesson. Avoid: permissive-by-default validation; deciding the funding model after commercial adoption.**

### A.8.3 Wolfram Language / Mathematica

The existence proof that one typed symbolic substrate can span curated entities, units, symbolic math, and numerics across all four sciences — and the near-perfect inverse of the product on every audit axis. A Wolfram result can be neither audited (closed algorithms; implementation notes give "only a rough indication"; reverse engineering contractually banned) nor replayed (Knowledgebase unversioned; the official academic citation guidance is to record *the time of the query* "as it can affect the result" — an admission of irreproducibility as policy); `Quantity` heuristically auto-parses unknown unit strings — silent defaulting at the exact point the product fails closed. Curation doctrine, from Wolfram's own retrospective: crowd-sourcing was tried and abandoned; "there always has to be an expert involved"; data usable for computation over *all* members of a class (his level 10) is categorically more expensive than question-answering data. **Adopt: the Quantity dimensional discipline (absolute-vs-difference temperature splits), the entity-typed vocabulary shape, the curation-cost honesty. Avoid: the trust-me black box; single-vendor vocabulary — rented, unforkable, mortal.**

### A.8.4 SageMath

The cautionary tale for the orchestrator half. One typed surface over ~90+ backends worked *as a type system* (the parent/coercion spine with canonical morphisms is worth copying) and failed *as a distribution*: ~442 vendored packages, patches against ~63, multi-hour builds, Debian removal for unmaintainability, and a wrapper layer of 700k+ lines whose largest subsystem exists to reconcile backends' incompatible equality/simplification models — with the founder's own 2014 verdict "overall failed" against the replace-Magma/Maple/Mathematica/Matlab mission (sage-devel, accessed 2026-08-26). Semantic mismatches (branch cuts, simplification domains) were wrapped, never eliminated; failure semantics are untyped exceptions; reproducibility was pinned per-universe rather than per-result. **Adopt: coercion-with-explicit-canonical-morphisms; doctests-as-conformance-corpus. Avoid: becoming a distribution; text/pexpect adapters without typed contracts; per-distribution instead of per-result pinning — Sage's missing mechanism is exactly the product's receipts.** `[inference: the prediction that adapters become the dominant permanent engineering surface for any orchestrator]`

### A.8.5 Lean 4 / mathlib

The strongest existing implementation of the product's `proved` verdict: small kernel, proof objects, independent re-checkers (leanchecker; lean4lean — which has found real kernel bugs), content-addressed builds, and a fail-closed elaboration discipline proven viable at 285k+ theorems. Two boundaries matter. Stating is cheap and vocabulary-limited; *proving* runs at a de Bruijn factor of ~4–10 and ~150–250 expert lines/day `[evidence-incomplete: figures from paper snippets]` — incompatible with a scientist asserting hundreds of claims daily. And the formalized analysis is **computationally inert**: mathlib's ℝ is noncomputable, Picard–Lindelöf is existence-only, kernel evaluation on ℝ is impossible — there is no path from a formalized ODE to a trajectory; SciLean, the bridge attempt, self-describes as early-stage. **Adopt: the trust architecture (small kernel + external checkers + plain-text export) as the shape of proof artifacts; Lean as an optional statement-level export target for the `proved` fragment. Avoid: coupling all knowledge to full proof obligation — the proof-cost wall is exactly why the product's `supported`/`refuted`/delegation verdicts must be first-class, not degraded proofs.**

### A.8.6 Isabelle/HOL + Coq/Rocq (units; validated numerics; certificates)

Two decisive teachings. (1) The AFP entry *Physical_Quantities* (Foster & Wolff 2020) encodes ISQ dimensions as type-level exponent vectors with same-dimension-only equality — proof that **unit soundness is a compile-time typing discipline; implement it in the product's own compiler, there is nothing to delegate**. (2) CoqInterval proves real-number bounds by *reflection*: the verified evaluator's execution is the proof, so **no transferable certificate exists** — an external product cannot consume assistant-internal numeric verdicts without re-trusting the assistant. What a Rust product *can* check independently: solver-native certificate formats where checking is cheap and the checker small — DRAT/LRAT for SAT (`rate`, an existing Rust/MIT checker), Alethe for SMT (`carcara`, existing Rust/Apache-2.0 checker/elaborator), SOS/Positivstellensatz identities and branch-and-bound subdivision traces for nonlinear bounds (the Flyspeck pattern) `[evidence-incomplete on Flyspeck specifics]`. Sledgehammer is the pattern for orchestration: untrusted external solvers as oracles, answers reconstructed through a small trusted core. **Adopt: verdict + solver-native certificate + small independent Rust checker per format; assistants as optional high-assurance auditors, never the runtime.**

### A.8.7 KeYmaera X

The working example of theorem proving over physical models, and a masterclass in TCB discipline: the KeYmaera→KeYmaera X rewrite shrank soundness-critical code from 65,989 lines to ~1,700–2,000 by making axioms concrete formulas instantiated by one uniform-substitution rule — adding reasoning power never grows the kernel (symbolaris.com, JAR 2017, accessed 2026-08-26). Its limits mark the honest boundary of "proving physics": polynomial/rational term language only, no PDEs, no stochastic dynamics, no units even after a decade (units live in an abandoned third-party fork), model assumptions implicit in formulas — and every proof bottoms out in a QE oracle (Mathematica, proprietary, in the TCB, unreceipted). Verdicts are conditional on an assumed-exact model; the product's typed assumptions and `out_of_regime` verdict are precisely the missing complement. **Adopt: the never-grow-the-kernel property; declared oracle trust boundaries; artifacts derived from proofs (ModelPlex monitors) as the adoption vector. Avoid: GPL+JVM distribution (caps adoption at "tool people visit"); expect that features not in the core type system from day one never arrive.**

### A.8.8 SBML + SED-ML + COMBINE archive (+ CellML)

The community's answer to "describe a model and its simulation so they replay" — structurally about half of the receipt story, behaviorally much less. What exists: SBML as a typed solver-neutral model IR with numbered validation rules; SED-ML naming algorithms and tolerances as KiSAO ontology terms; OMEX as the one-archive receipt container; BioSimulators as containerized conformance-tested backends. What is missing is exactly the product's territory: **units are optional and unit checks are warnings by explicit spec decision**; no content hashes, no solver implementation identity/version (KiSAO names the algorithm, not the binary), no mandatory seeds; no inference, verdicts, or typed failure taxonomy — and tools have been documented emitting SED-ML naming algorithms they did not use. The measured consequence of advisory semantics plus the 11-year model-format→experiment-format gap: **~half of 455 published models could not be reproduced from their publications** (Tiwari et al., Mol Syst Biol 2021). CellML's 2.0 amputated its reaction semantics — every widening beyond what implementations execute either stalled (SBML's decade-draft packages) or was cut. **Adopt: the OMEX shape from day one; KiSAO extended with implementation identity + hashes; BioSimulators' conformance machinery; SBML import/export as the ecosystem on-ramp. Avoid: advisory semantics — the strongest empirical argument for fail-closed in the whole survey.**

### A.8.9 COPASI / PySB / BioModels

The vocabulary-supply-chain evidence the §A.1(5) risk needed. Twenty years of institutional curation at EMBL-EBI produced a trusted core of ~1,000 models (~800 curated of ~2,000 submissions in 2020; 1,055 curated ODE models in 2025, 88% verified end-to-end *after* a repair campaign) against >140,000 uncurated auto-generated entries — because the load-bearing step, numerically reproducing published figures, "cannot be easily automated" (Smith et al., PLOS Comp Biol 2025). Even the curated corpus decayed: dangling model references, Infinity/NaN parameters, stale annotation URLs — name-based, after-the-fact, advisory provenance rotting exactly as hash-bound fail-closed ingest would have prevented. COPASI (Artistic-2.0, active) and Tellurium/libroadrunner are production simulation engines the product should orchestrate, never rebuild; Antimony is the precedent for a readable surface compiling to a standard IR; the 2025 five-engine cross-verification through pinned Docker containers with HDF5 numerical comparison is **a working community-scale prototype of reproducibility receipts**. **Adopt: cross-engine comparison as the honesty mechanism; the Antimony pattern. Avoid: volume beside verification without a hard typed distinction.**

### A.8.10 CWL / Nextflow / Snakemake / WDL

**Roughly 70–80% of the orchestration checklist is already solved and must be adopted or emitted, not re-implemented** `[inference, from the mechanism inventory]`: containerized invocation, caching/resume (Nextflow's per-task hash over inputs+script+container+environment; Snakemake's Merkle-tree cache), HPC/cloud scheduling with per-task partial results, and provenance capture (CWLProv research objects; nf-prov emitting Workflow Run RO-Crate). CWL's typed IO contract — File/Directory/record/enum/union types, fail-closed input readiness, container-as-part-of-the-declared-unit — is the best-designed piece and the natural emission target for the product's batch tier. What all four fundamentally lack: any semantics of the computation. Types stop at bytes (a task expecting km type-checks receiving miles); success is an exit code; caching is byte-identity, never problem-identity; tolerances and seeds are undifferentiated parameters, so the product's disposition taxonomy is inexpressible. Sobering history: CWL — permissive, well-typed, conformance-tested — froze at v1.2 for six years, its provenance arm single-engine; the lesson is **a specification without a compelling runtime freezes, and portability alone is not a value proposition.** **Adopt: compile solver obligations down to CWL/WDL documents; copy the task-hash design but key it on canonical-IR hashes. Avoid: burning the solo year on schedulers and container plumbing — solved problems.**

### A.8.11 W3C PROV / RO-Crate / WholeTale / Nix / Guix

The receipts substrate decision, largely settled by the survey: **adopt Nix (or Guix) closure pinning inside the receipt; export receipts as PROV-O/RO-Crate at the boundary; build the integrity layer yourself, because nothing here provides it.** PROV standardizes graph shapes (Entity/Activity/Agent, seven relations) with no canonical serialization for hashing, no signing, no binding between the asserted graph and what ran — testimony, not receipts. RO-Crate (active: 1.3.0 released 2026-06-22) explicitly delegates integrity to optional BagIt checksums, its own notes conceding the manifest "does not provide proof the RO-Crate has not been deliberately tampered with." Nix pins what a container digest cannot — the recipe's complete input closure, rebuildable and auditable — while the digest pins byte-identical runtime; **a receipt should carry both**. WholeTale is the memento mori: an NSF-funded hosted reproducibility platform, dashboard now 404, repos archived — reproducibility coupled to a platform has the lifetime of a funding cycle; **receipts must verify offline with a local library.** (Locally convenient: Nibli's flake-pinned dev environment means the substrate is already in the product's toolchain `[local-fact]`.)

### A.8.12 OpenMath / Content MathML (failed universalism I)

Adoption honesty: the official CD repository has been dormant since 2022-06-28; browsers never implemented content markup and MathML Core formally excludes it; living consumers are the MathML 4 spec itself, GAP's SCSCP niche, and Wolfram pinned at MathML 2.0. Failure mechanism, in causal order: **(1) no binding to computational semantics** — conformance was encoding-level, formal properties were unchecked prose-adjacent metadata, so implementing OpenMath bought syntax interchange without meaning interchange (Fateman's 2001 critique predicted exactly this); **(2) the phrasebook burden** — N systems × M dictionaries of translation code written by grant-funded third parties, dead with the grants; **(3) CD governance froze** while real semantics scattered into private dictionaries. The lesson for any "universal scientific IR," stated as mechanism: *an IR without an owned, checkable evaluation semantics is a serialization format, and serialization formats survive only where a deployment channel forces them.* **The product must own semantics end-to-end — one compiler, adapters it writes and tests itself, interchange as an export, not the product. Never let a vocabulary entry exist whose semantics is only a description string.**

### A.8.13 OWL / OBO Foundry / DL reasoners (failed universalism II — half of it)

The heading "failed universalism" is only half right, and the halves separate cleanly along *what a classifier can conclude*. The genuine, under-celebrated success: the **identifier contract** — stable opaque IRIs under governed PURL namespaces, mandatory definitions, deprecation-not-deletion, changed-meaning-requires-new-term (OBO P19) — arguably the most successful scientific-KR infrastructure ever shipped; tens of thousands of curators and virtually every genomics database consume these identifiers daily. The failure: computational payoff of the *semantics*. A DL classifier computes subsumption hierarchies, satisfiability, and subsumption-closed retrieval — full stop; everything scientists actually compute (enrichment statistics, kinetics, differential equations) is arithmetic or statistical, which subsumption cannot express; the expressive tier's reasoners (HermiT) are a decade unmaintained while only PTIME EL survived, and GO's recommended artifact is the deliberately logic-weakened `go-basic` DAG. The largest payoff attributed to GO — enrichment analysis — uses it as a plain graph; the DL contributes nothing at query time `[inference, argued in the evidence file]`. **Adopt: bind to existing ChEBI/GO/NCBI identifiers rather than minting competitors; the governance dashboard pattern; validation-before-materialization as compiler semantics; a tractable always-on logical fragment with heavier reasoning explicitly budgeted. Avoid: open-world monotonic binary-property semantics for empirical claims; promising that classification computes science.**

### A.8.14 Stan / PyMC / ArviZ + GUM (critic addition — the uncertainty axis)

Materially corrects this report's novelty claim. Stan is shipped prior art for a typed declarative surface with fail-closed compilation (six ordered blocks, declaration-before-use, constrained types, a separate typecheck phase — the product's pipeline shape, in production since 2012) and for **machine-readable numerical diagnostics as a de facto disposition record** (divergences, R-hat, ESS, treedepth in ArviZ's documented InferenceData schema — which the product's receipts should embed or emit, not rival). Per uncertainty kind: parameter uncertainty is *solved* (posteriors — the state of the art to match); numerical is *diagnosed but not propagated*; measurement is representable only as an authorial idiom the tooling cannot see; structural is *absent* (one program is one model; comparison is post hoc, and stacking-vs-BMA shows it has no settled semantics in the M-open case). Two warnings the product must carry: **Gelman's folk theorem — computational failure is usually evidence of model misspecification — is the claim that `non_convergent` (disposition) and `invalid_model` (verdict-adjacent) are empirically entangled**, exactly where the two-axis taxonomy will be stressed; and the survey's sharpest flag: *no fetched source demonstrates any system keeping the four uncertainty kinds distinct through inference* — the product's separation claim is **untested, not just unshipped** (addressed in A.5.10). What GUM/JCGM adds that Stan omits: Type A/B evaluation as a provenance tag per uncertainty component, uncertainty budgets, coverage factors, and JCGM 101 as an existing normative Monte Carlo propagation spec — the metrology vocabulary the measurement-uncertainty type should bind to. **Adopt: constrained-type discipline; InferenceData; GUM binding. Avoid: leaving workflow discipline outside the language — the 77-page Bayesian Workflow paper is practice its own authors could not formalize into software; convention demonstrably does not hold.**

### A.8.15 Dakota + ASME V&V/UQ programme (critic addition — the orchestrator-with-uncertainty precedent)

The shipped instance of black-box solver orchestration with typed uncertainty, at national-lab scale for 30 years. Two mechanisms to adopt wholesale: the **parameter/response black-box contract** (zero modification of the simulation code — the entire adoption story — with explicit failure-capturing semantics mapping onto the disposition axis), and the **aleatory/epistemic type distinction** — distributions vs intervals/Dempster–Shafer structures as structurally different declared kinds, mixed problems propagated as nested loops yielding p-boxes, never one collapsed number: a debugged, published semantics. ASME V&V 20's `u_val = sqrt(u_num² + u_input² + u_D²)` keeps numerical/input/experimental uncertainty as separate budget components, and its validation-point scoping is the doctrinal precedent for `out_of_regime` — but the validation domain is a *report-level* concept; no machine artifact encodes it. The forty-year finding that most challenges this product: **the formal pieces all exist and are debugged; what never existed is a user population willing to re-author its models in a checkable language.** Dakota won by asking nothing semantic of its users; a fail-closed language asks everything. **The product's wedge is models small enough to re-author, and the typed layer must pay for itself immediately — compile errors that catch real mistakes, receipts a reviewer accepts — or it loses to input-deck culture on friction alone.**

### A.8.16 Cyc (critic addition — the feasibility precedent)

The strongest cautionary precedent for a small team hand-curating cross-domain formal knowledge with inference: ~$200M and ~2,000 person-years over 40 years `[evidence-incomplete on exact figures]`, ending as hospital revenue-cycle software — the KB never repaid its curation. But Cyc also ships the one mechanism nothing else in the survey has: **microtheories** — assertion bundles scoped by declared domain assumptions functioning as factored-out conjuncts, first-class contexts in a specialization hierarchy with explicit *lifting* rules for cross-context import, and local-only consistency so rival contradictory models coexist. This is the direct prior art for A.5's contexts, and it yields a beautiful formalization this report adopts: **`out_of_regime` is a failed lift** — a query whose supporting assertions cannot be soundly imported into the query's declared context; and structural uncertainty *requires* a context mechanism, because no type system lets contradictory axiom sets coexist `[proposal, adopted into A.5.10]`. The design corrections Cyc teaches: make lifting explicit, fail-closed, deterministic (Cyc's defeasible argumentation made cross-context outcomes unauditable); keep one small sound core (Cyc's 1,100+ accreted heuristic reasoners, with the general prover secretly disabled after a million consecutive timeouts, meant no independent semantics and no possibility of a differential oracle); ship the interchange seam and permissive license first (killing OpenCyc killed the only path to becoming infrastructure); and **curate only vocabulary that external formal standards already define, so every entry has a mechanizable oracle** — the transcription-not-introspection inversion that makes a solo four-science vocabulary even conceivable.

### A.8.17 TPTP / SZS / SMT-LIB / CASC / SMT-COMP (critic addition — the taxonomy and certificate precedent)

Materially corrects a second novelty claim: **the verdict/disposition split already shipped, two decades ago, as the SZS ontology** — a semantic *success* sub-ontology (Theorem, CounterSatisfiable, Unsatisfiable, ContradictoryAxioms, Unknown, …) crossed with a *no-success* sub-ontology (Timeout, MemoryOut, GaveUp, Error, Incomplete), machine-parsable, used by every CASC entrant. SMT-LIB adds fail-closed sorted declarations, `set-logic` as the static ancestor of regime declaration, and `(get-info :reason-unknown)` as a disposition channel. The product should adopt these shapes and inherit two hard-won findings: (1) **certificate formats never converged** — DRAT succeeded in SAT only because clausal proofs need one rule family; SMT proof formats fragment per solver, the Alethe spec is officially "evolving," and SMT-COMP's proof-exhibition track refused to define a valid proof and disappeared after 2023 — so the realistic seam is *per-backend certificates with the receipt recording certificate format + checker identity + checker verdict (including "holey")*, never one mandated format; (2) **disagreement is the working honesty oracle** — planted known-status problems, cross-solver majority voting on cores, independent model validation, published disagreements, publicly committed seeds, scrambled inputs, retrospective disqualification: the competition machinery is the product's backend-admission protocol in mature form. Taxonomy consequences adopted in A.4: SZS classifies `ContradictoryAxioms` as a *success* (a semantic determination), supporting treating provable model inconsistency as a first-class outcome; and SZS's `GaveUp`/`Incomplete` motivates an honest-incompleteness disposition the prompt's list lacks.

**Because every one of its constituent mechanisms exists, shipped and debugged, in a different system that cannot host the others — and every prior attempt at the composition failed for an identifiable, avoidable reason.** The decomposition, with the shipped instance of each mechanism: fail-closed typed declarative compilation (Stan, SMT-LIB, Lean); units as compile-time types (Isabelle AFP; Wolfram's Quantity, minus the fail-open parsing); verdict/disposition result taxonomy (SZS); typed uncertainty kinds with non-collapse (Dakota's aleatory/epistemic; GUM's budgets; V&V 20's u_val decomposition); assumption-scoped contexts and regimes (Cyc microtheories; V&V validation domains); proof objects with independent checking (Lean/KeYmaera kernels; DRAT/Alethe checkers); solver orchestration with typed IO, caching, containers (CWL/Nextflow; Dakota's black-box contract; Sledgehammer); replayable experiment description (SED-ML/OMEX; BioSimulators); environment pinning (Nix); provenance interchange (PROV/RO-Crate); vocabulary governance (OBO's identifier contract). `[inference — the synthesis; each constituent is external-fact per the evidence files]`

## A.9 §0.2 Scope challenge

Method: two independent agents argued the narrowest defensible v1 and the four-domain case respectively, each grounded in the survey digests and each required to steelman and to name its own weakest point (full arguments archived at `research/stage-a-evidence/debate-narrow.md` and `debate-broad.md`); the lead architect judges here. The single most load-bearing observation: **both sides independently converged on the same implemented v1 — chemistry⇄biology as biochemical reaction networks** — and the residual disagreement is only whether the *IR is specified* four-domain-wide before one pair is implemented. That convergence, from adversarial starting positions, is the strongest internal evidence in this report. `[inference]`

### The three §0.2 questions

**1. Which pair generates the most value from co-residence, and what is lost shipping only it?** Chemistry + biology, instantiated as biochemical reaction networks with typed measurement evidence. The co-residence value is mechanical, not thematic: chemistry-side invariants — governed species identity (ChEBI-bound), stoichiometric mass/charge balance, thermodynamic consistency — only function as *compile-time checks* on biology-side models and evidence if both live in one compiler; separated, they degrade to conventions, and the survey measured what conventions cost (advisory SBML units; ~50% published-model irreproducibility; BioModels' curation debt — A.8.8/9). This is also the pair where **every differentiator lands on a documented, quantified unmet need** and where each fix has a copyable precedent rather than an invention (Antimony, OMEX, KiSAO, BioSimulators). Math+physics, by contrast, is the maximal-incumbent pair: rebuilding Modelica/MTK's decades of DAE compiler engineering to reach users those tools already serve, dragging in frames and PDEs — the two most expensive KR features — into a market that buys speed, not audit. What is lost, stated without flinching: the entire physics/engineering audience; general symbolic mathematics; cross-domain queries spanning physics and biology; stochastic/spatial regimes (deferred, disclosed via `out_of_regime`/`unsupported` rather than simulated badly); the four-domain headline demo. The addressable v1 community shrinks to systems biology / pharmacometrics — small, but the one community with a governed-vocabulary culture (OBO), regulatory pressure toward auditability, and a quantified reproducibility wound. `[proposal, adopting the convergent debate outcome]`

**2. What is different now that did not hold for the prior attempts?** Four things, in decreasing order of evidential strength. (a) **Composition has replaced invention**: every surveyed pioneer built its whole stack; today each hard sub-mechanism has a shipped, adoptable artifact (the A.8.18 inventory), so the product is an integration compiler over existing seams — a category that did not exist when Modelica, SBML, or Cyc started `[inference from the survey]`. (b) **The reproducibility crisis is institutionalized and measured** — the 50%-irreproducibility number is what forced BioModels' repair campaign; funders and journals now demand what only receipt-emitting tools produce `[external-fact for the measurements; inference for the demand trend]`. (c) **Rust + WASM components give one codebase for linkable library, service, and sandboxed solver seam** — the typed plug boundary FMI needed a C ABI for `[inference]`. (d) **Agent labor collapses the two costs that killed unified breadth** — library/adapter authoring and corpus curation — while fail-closed compilation is precisely what makes agent-generated content safe to accept (candidates cheap, gate sound); and LLM-era machine-written science creates a new buyer for a deterministic verifier. **(d) is a hypothesis with no shipped counterexample either way, and the report flags it as such rather than resting on it** `[open]`. Honesty requires noting what is *not* different: the adoption barrier (users must re-author models — Dakota's forty years) and the fail-closed-asks-everything friction are unchanged, and they are the two most probable causes of failure.

**3. What is the smallest scope that still requires the recommended architecture?** The yeast vertical slice itself, plus its rejection corpus. It already requires the compiler (fail-closed units/identity/context checking, regime validation), the orchestrator (two distinct solver capabilities — exact stoichiometric linear algebra and correlated uncertainty propagation, with delegated ODE simulation as the third arriving immediately after), and receipts with replay. A well-documented script could compute the yeast *numbers*; it could not fail closed on a missing water-vapour declaration, produce a basis-citing `refuted`-for-this-model, refuse out-of-regime queries, or replay under a declared bound — **the architecture is required by the guarantees, not by the arithmetic. The corollary is the falsifier: if target users do not value the guarantees over the script, there is no product** (this is the build/don't-build criterion, restated in A.11). `[proposal]`

### Judgment: the narrowing decision `[proposal]`

**Narrow for v1 — the prompt's explicitly acceptable outcome, taken.** Concretely:

- **Implemented v1 domain scope:** biochemical reaction networks — chemistry (governed species identity, stoichiometry, mass/charge balance) + biology (networks, deterministic kinetics, organism/strain/regime context, dose-response-class evidence) — on a mathematical substrate (exact rational arithmetic and linear algebra, interval enclosures, first-order correlated uncertainty propagation) with **physics-of-measurement as infrastructure** (units/dimensions, gas-law-class model laws with regime declarations, CODATA constants as versioned imported evidence). Mathematics and physics are *served domains* in no v1 sense; they are load-bearing plumbing. The yeast slice sits exactly on this scope by construction.
- **The envelope is specified domain-generically; the domain IRs are not.** The broad debater's "four-domain IR, one-pair implementation" is adopted only for the layer where it is true by construction — quantities/units, uncertainty kinds, claim kinds, contexts/regimes, verdict×disposition, receipts are domain-generic machinery, and specifying them narrowly would be malpractice. It is **rejected** for the domain IRs themselves: no physics IR (frames, tensors, fields) and no mathematics IR beyond the substrate will be designed in Stage B — the broad debater's own weakest-point concedes that a spec exercised by no implementation is "a design intention wearing a spec," the exact aspirations-as-shipped sin the programme forbids. Stage B therefore produces full §6/§7 (chemistry/biology) designs plus §4/§5 (math/physics) *requirements analyses with explicit not-designed markers*, satisfying the prompt's structure without faking coverage.
- **Expansion is a typed refusal, not a silent absence:** unimplemented domains return `unsupported{planned|out_of_scope}` naming the capability — the taxonomy is the scoping mechanism, which is what makes narrowing honest where the survey's narrowings (SBML packages, CellML reactions, CWL freeze) were silent, late, or amputations.
- **Architecture consequence:** nothing in the narrowing removes the compiler/orchestrator/receipts architecture (question 3), so the scope is not an excuse for a lesser design — and conversely, per §0.2's over-building test, no *additional* architecture is licensed by domains v1 does not serve.

**Build vs don't-build (the §0.4 option-4 answer at Stage A depth):** the survey supports *build*: no existing system composes the mechanisms (A.8.18), each buy-or-wrap candidate fails for a specific structural reason, and the pieces worth buying (FMI, CWL, OMEX, SZS shapes, Nix, OBO identifiers, InferenceData, GUM) are adoptable *inside* a build. Evidence that would flip this to don't-build, pre-registered now: (i) SSP Traceability or an unseen platform layer turning out to already ship machine-checked, hash-bound receipts over typed models (A.8.18's flagged assumption); (ii) Stage B failing to design the four-way uncertainty separation coherently (Stan's warning — the one *feasibility* falsifier); (iii) the authoring study (the roadmap's adoption ceiling) showing target users will not re-author models even for receipts (Dakota's forty-year barrier); (iv) the narrow debater's sustainability point hardening — if validated mechanisms would simply be absorbed by incumbents, the right product might be a *standard plus reference checker*, not a platform. `[proposal]`

*Confidence: medium-high — the pair choice rests on adversarial convergence plus quantified survey evidence; the "what is different now" answer is honest about which legs are hypotheses. Largest unverified assumption: that the systems-biology/pharmacometrics community's documented reproducibility pain translates into willingness to adopt a fail-closed authoring language — the Dakota precedent argues culture beats mechanism, and no user evidence exists yet on either side (personas are invented; §A.1(2)).*
