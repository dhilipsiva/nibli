## system

Cyc / Cycorp — CycL, the microtheory (context) mechanism, and the 1984–present hand-curation program (MCC 1984 → Cycorp 1994 → post-Lenat Cycorp, Austin TX)

## overlap

Cyc is the closest historical attempt at the product's KR+inference core, at mechanism level. (1) Typed-ish declarative surface: CycL is full FOPC plus higher-order and modal extensions, n-ary predicates (not OWL's binary straitjacket), and term-constructing functions ((LiquidFn Nitrogen)) — the same "predicate calls over a curated vocabulary" shape as the proposed surface language, with every axiom hand-checked "for default correctness, generality, and best placement" (Lenat & Marcus 2023), a human analog of fail-closed curation. (2) Assumption-scoped truth: every assertion lives in a microtheory whose domain assumptions act as factored-out implicit conjuncts ("Fluorine is a gas" under normal-Earth-surface conditions); microtheories are first-class terms arranged in a specialization hierarchy, composed by functions like IntersectContexts (~10,000 named contexts), with explicit lifting rules governing when P true in C1 supports Q in C2 — the direct ancestor of "declared model assumptions and reference frames". (3) Proof objects and provenance: every conclusion carries justification chains bottoming out in "told to be true in context C by source X at time Y", with step-by-step auditable traces and NLG explanations — the product's provenance-receipt ambition, minus hashes. (4) Verdict nuance: Cyc returns pro- and con-arguments, prefers specific over general, and when strong arguments are incommensurable it "decides not to trust that statement or its negation" — a shipped analog of the proved/supported/conflicting/unknown axis. (5) Orchestration: inference is a "conversation" among 1,100+ specialized Heuristic-Level reasoners that bid on subproblems and are interrupted on budget overrun — structurally the solver-orchestrator pattern, including resource_exhausted-style cutoffs, applied to logical rather than numerical backends. (6) Local-consistency contexts let Cyc hold contradictory expert models simultaneously and answer under each — exactly the competing-model workflow the product needs for structural uncertainty.

## insufficiency

Missing mechanisms, not ambitions: (1) No checked quantity types — CycL has argument constraints via isa/genls collections but no units, dimensions, or reference frames as compile-time-checked types; "missing units" cannot be a compile error in a language where almost every axiom is default-true. (2) No uncertainty representation at all: assertions are default-true or monotonically-true, with no measurement/numerical/parameter/structural uncertainty channels, no error propagation, no numeric confidence (deliberately). (3) No typed numerical delegation: the 1,100 HL modules are logic shortcuts (transitivity lookups, specialized graph closures), not bridges to ODE/linear-algebra/chemistry solvers with tolerances, seeds, and convergence reporting. (4) No determinism or replay: which HL module answers depends on a bidding/timeout scheduler; Lenat's own footnote admits the general theorem prover timed out on "over a million queries in a row" and was "quietly turned off" a decade before 2023 — the semantics of a query is whatever the surviving heuristics compute, which is the opposite of deterministic compilation and makes an independent proof checker impossible. (5) Unsound by design: "even deduction in Cyc is not sound" (Cyc Technology Overview) because nearly all knowledge is defeasible; conclusions are argumentation outcomes, not derivations a third party can re-verify. (6) No reproducibility receipts: provenance is KB-internal metadata, not content-addressed source/IR/artifact hashes with solver identities and replay instructions. (7) No interchange seam: no stable public IR ever shipped; the OWL export (OpenCyc) was lossy and discontinued. (8) No disposition axis: resource cutoffs, backend absence, and non-convergence are not surfaced as typed outcomes distinct from verdicts.

## narrowing_history

Cyc narrowed continuously and documentably. 1984 launch goal: encode enough consensus common sense that the "knowledge pump" would prime itself by ~1995 and learn autonomously; Lenat predicted that by 2000 "no one would even think about having a computer that doesn't have Cyc running on it" (yuxi.ml history, citing period sources). By 2001 the AGI deadline had slipped to 2020–2025. The 2000s Semantic-Web positioning (Cyc as reasoning backbone), the FACTory crowdsourcing game (2005–2012), and the EU Large Knowledge Collider (2008–2011) all ended without deliverables. Around 2014–2016 Cycorp removed most public documentation (tutorials, vocabulary references) from the web; OpenCyc was shut down in early 2017 with a statement that it was "fragmenting" the brand; ResearchCyc distribution ended around 2019 without announcement. Lenat declared Cyc "done" as a product (not an AGI) around 2016, and commercialization narrowed to enterprise consulting (Cleveland Clinic clinical-trial recruitment, energy-sector advisors). After Lenat's death (2023-08-31), the narrowing endpoint is visible on cyc.com today: Cyc is marketed as healthcare enterprise AI for "200+ hospitals" — charge capture, denials management, post-acute forecasting. The cause pattern in the record: hand-curation costs never crossed the autonomy threshold they were supposed to fund; closed development prevented an ecosystem from carrying the breadth ambition when Cycorp couldn't; and the defeasible-heuristic inference core could not produce the verifiable value that would have justified the KB's cost outside bespoke consulting.

## adoption

Honest reality: after ~40 years Cyc is infrastructure nowhere. Observable evidence gathered this task: cyc.com (2026-08-26) markets exclusively to hospital revenue-cycle operations, claiming "200+ hospitals" — a vendor claim I could not independently verify; no developer ecosystem exists (no public API, no registry packages, docs purged 2014–2016); the OpenCyc line ended 2017 and survives only as archival mirrors of a 2012 OWL dump; ResearchCyc is gone. Third-party trackers (Tracxn/RocketReach, seen only as search snippets — evidence-incomplete) put Cycorp at ~43 employees and ~$5M revenue. The yuxi.ml history (2025) concludes all known commercial uses were "functionally the same as similar services offered by established corporations like Oracle and IBM", i.e., expert-systems/data-integration consulting, with Cycorp achieving unusual small-company financial stability but no platform adoption. Named historical customers: GlaxoSmithKline (2001), Cleveland Clinic (2007–2010s), Lycos (to 2001), CycSecure (2002). Academic adoption collapsed after OpenCyc's death; the Lenat & Marcus 2023 paper is a plea for relevance in the LLM era, and its own text concedes Cyc is "often not deep and broad enough… not fast enough".

## lessons

Copy: (1) The microtheory design itself — assertion bundles scoped by declared domain assumptions, contexts as first-class queryable objects in a specialization partial order, context-composition functions instead of reifying every combination, and explicit lifting rules for cross-context import; this is the only shipped mechanism matching "model assumptions and reference frames scope validity". (2) Per-assertion provenance bottoming out in source+context+time. (3) Pro/con outcome honesty — refusing to trust either side of an incommensurable conflict maps to the product's `conflicting` verdict. (4) Budgeted orchestration of specialized backends with interruption — but surfaced as typed dispositions, which Cyc never did. Avoid: (1) Default-true logic as the substrate — it cost Cyc soundness, determinism, and checkable proofs; the product's fail-closed monotonic core with explicit uncertainty types is the direct antithesis and should stay that way. (2) Inference-by-accreted-heuristics: 20 reasoners (1989) → 1,100+ (2023) with the general prover secretly disabled means no independent semantics, no differential oracle, no reimplementation — keep one small verified core and push speed into refusable, receipt-bearing backends. (3) Closed development as a survival strategy: killing OpenCyc/ResearchCyc to stop "fragmenting" killed the only channel by which Cyc could have become infrastructure; ship the interchange seam and permissive license first, not as an afterthought. (4) Hand-curating open-ended commonsense: curate only vocabulary that external formal standards (SI, CODATA, IUPAC, established theory) already define, so each entry has a mechanizable oracle rather than an ontologist's judgment.

## cluster_answer

MECHANIZATION. A CycL assertion is never globally true; it is asserted in a microtheory, a first-class term. Each microtheory carries domain assumptions that function "as conjuncts for each of the assertions in that context" (Lenat & Marcus 2023) — stated once, inherited by everything inside, keeping assertions terse and reasoning local. Microtheories form a specialization hierarchy (CanadianProfessionalHockey specializes CanadianSports, ProfessionalSports, Hockey, Post1900, RealWorld): assertions in more general contexts are visible in specializations, and combinations are built by context-valued functions (IntersectContexts) rather than by reifying the product space (~10,000 named contexts as of 2023). Cross-context transfer is governed by lifting: rules that answer "if P holds in C1 and P⇒Q holds in C2, in which C3 may Q be concluded?", optionally adding decontextualizing clauses when an assertion is exported to a context lacking its home assumptions. Conflict is a feature: only local consistency is required; contradictory microtheories (rival experts, StarWars vs RealWorld, alternative planning scenarios) coexist, and query-time argumentation with meta-rules (specificity preference) adjudicates, refusing to trust either side when strong arguments are incommensurable. CONTEXT VS TYPES. Types suffice for per-term invariants: units, dimensions, and a frame tag on a single quantity are properties of a value and belong in the type system. A context mechanism is needed wherever validity is a property of a set of statements under shared background premises: regime bounds (Newtonian vs relativistic, dilute-solution rate laws, ideal-gas ranges), in-vitro/in-vivo scoping, temporal validity of empirical claims, and above all structural uncertainty — holding two mutually inconsistent models of one phenomenon and evaluating the same query under each. No type discipline lets contradictory axiom sets coexist; that requires scoped assertion bundles plus a controlled import relation. The product's `out_of_regime` verdict is precisely a failed lift: a query whose supporting assertions cannot be soundly imported into the query's declared context. The design correction Cyc teaches: make lifting explicit, fail-closed, and deterministic (a missing lift is a compile/inference error), not defeasible-argumentation as Cyc did, which made cross-context outcomes unauditable. CURATION ECONOMICS AND CLOSED INFERENCE. First, a premise correction: the record supports roughly two person-millennia, not hundreds — ~600 person-years/$60M by 2002 (Wikipedia), ~2,000 person-years/~$200M/30M assertions total (yuxi.ml, 2025; consistent with Lenat & Marcus's "a few million person-hours"), at ~$5/assertion falling to ~$0.7. That is still the strongest cautionary precedent the survey lacks: even at industrial scale and heroic per-assertion cost reduction, hand-curated breadth never reached the self-sustaining threshold, and the payoff surface (consulting engagements) never repaid the KB. What specifically blocked adoption: (a) authoring required months-trained ontologists writing an idiosyncratic formalism with judgment-based placement — no path for outside contributors; (b) no interchange seam survived: OpenCyc was a lossy OWL shadow killed in 2017 explicitly to prevent "fragmenting", ResearchCyc died ~2019, docs were purged 2014–2016, so nothing could build on Cyc without Cycorp; (c) inference was closed twice over — proprietary and epistemically opaque: 1,100+ accreted heuristic modules, defeasible truth, a scheduler-dependent answer path, and a general prover silently disabled after a million consecutive timeouts, so no third party could verify, reimplement, or trust its semantics; (d) business-model lock: enterprise consulting monetized the KB's scarcity, structurally opposed to becoming infrastructure. FOR THE FEASIBILITY CONCLUSION: Cyc says a solo developer cannot hand-curate open-ended cross-domain knowledge — but the proposed product's viability rests on three deliberate inversions: its vocabulary is the formal core of four sciences, where entries are transcribed from existing standards (SI, CODATA, IUPAC, textbook theory) with mechanizable oracles, not invented by introspection; its core is monotonic, sound, deterministic, and differentially testable, so trust does not depend on trusting the vendor; and it ships permissive licensing plus interchange from day one. Where the design repeats Cyc — any drift toward judgment-curated breadth, accreted special-case reasoners, or a closed KB as the moat — the Cyc record predicts the same end: forty years of curation, then hospital billing.
