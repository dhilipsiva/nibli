## thesis

The four-domain scope is defensible for v1 because every documented narrowing in the survey was a failure of implementation breadth, unaudited semantics, or business model — never of a typed multi-domain representation — and 2026 is the first moment when the domain-generic machinery is a composition problem over shipped standards rather than eleven research programs; therefore ship a four-domain IR with a one-pair implementation, and make declining the other domains a typed verdict instead of a silent retreat.

## argument

Read the survey for what actually narrowed. Not one of the eleven systems narrowed because a typed cross-domain representation failed. Modelica's retreat was commercial: source-level model exchange between competing vendors died, while its minimal typed seam — FMI's small C API plus typed XML metadata — won 280+ tools against ~a dozen full-language implementations. Sage "overall failed" (Stein's own 2014 words) at reimplementing four commercial systems' computation, not at its coercion/parent type spine, which worked so well the survey's own recommendation is to copy it. Wolfram never narrowed computationally; it became unauditable (Reduce: ~350 pages of WL plus ~1,400 pages of closed C, decompilation prohibited) and curation-priced. OpenMath had fail-closed vocabulary in its very syntax and still failed — because compliance was defined at encoding level and semantics were never machine-checked. SBML's unit checks are warnings (the 105xx series) by explicit decision, not necessity. OWL narrowed to EL because SROIQ was intractable — and the fix that worked was typed, declared fragments, which is exactly what a verdict/disposition taxonomy generalizes. The pattern across the whole survey: breadth of representation survived everywhere it was seriously tried; what died was breadth of implementation, silent semantic gaps, and monetization. The prior-art graveyard does not contain this product's failure mode.

Second, standards maturity converts invention into composition. Every surveyed pioneer built its entire stack. Today each hard sub-problem has a shipped, adoptable artifact: FMI 3.0's typed-capability solver seam; CWL's fail-closed typed IO contract ("ready when all required inputs have concrete values") plus existing engines for the batch tier; SBML/SED-ML/OMEX plus KiSAO naming solver algorithms and tolerances as ontology terms; BioSimulators' pinned containerized solvers with cross-engine numerical comparison — a working implementation of reproducibility receipts; PROV-O/RO-Crate as the receipt export vocabulary; Nix/Guix for bit-reproducible environments; OBO's identifier contract (P19: changed meaning = new ID) with ChEBI/GO/Uberon as governed vocabularies to bind rather than curate; the Isabelle AFP units entry proving dimensional soundness belongs in the compiler; Sledgehammer's oracles-re-checked-by-a-kernel discipline and KeYmaera's ~2,000-line kernel as the trust shape. The proposed product is an integration compiler over these seams — the first attempt for which "four domains" means binding four vocabularies to one type system, not funding four research programs.

Third, tooling and the LLM era change the labor equation the survey says was decisive. Rust gives one codebase for linkable library, service, and sandboxed WASM solver components — a typed, deterministic plug seam FMI needed a C ABI for and CWL needs containers to approximate. On demand: the reproducibility crisis is institutionalized (the 50% irreproducibility figure is what forced BioModels' assurance retreat; funders and journals now mandate what only receipt-emitting tools can produce), and LLM-generated science creates a new buyer for exactly one thing — a fail-closed deterministic verifier with proof objects that machine-written claims can be compiled against. On supply: the two costs that killed unified breadth — library authoring (mathlib's 285,767 theorems, Wolfram's curation staff) and corpus curation — are precisely the costs agent labor collapses, and fail-closed compilation is what makes agent-generated breadth safe to accept: candidates are cheap, the gate is sound.

Now the strongest counterargument, head-on: solo developer, twelve months, and a survey where everyone narrowed. Conceded as a constraint on implementation; denied as a constraint on representation. The honest strongest case is exactly four-domain IR, one-pair implementation. The domain-generic layer — quantities/units/dimensions, the four uncertainty kinds as distinct types, the verdict×disposition taxonomy, hash-bound receipts — is one body of machinery: a chemistry claim and a physics claim differ in vocabulary bindings, not in type-system features. Specifying it four-wide is cheap; populating it is what is expensive, so v1 populates one vertical and declines the rest loudly. This is where the taxonomy is not a reporting nicety but the scoping mechanism itself: `unsupported` and `out_of_regime` make unimplemented physics a typed verdict rather than a silent default. Every ugly narrowing in the survey was silent, late, or dishonest — SBML claiming models while units stayed advisory, BioModels certifying uncurated models, OpenMath conforming without meaning. A system that types its own limits converts narrowing from a retreat into a roadmap, and the survey shows honest minimal seams (FMI, RO-Crate, Antimony) are the ones that got adopted. Twelve months of solo-plus-agents work cannot build four validated domain libraries; it can build one sound spine, one deep pair that exercises every differentiator, and three conformance-tested vocabulary stubs whose refusals are verdicts. That is not the ambition every surveyed system abandoned — it is the shape each of them, in its documented "copy" lessons, retroactively wishes it had shipped first.

## recommended_v1_scope

Spec-complete and conformance-tested across all four domains: the typed IR; fail-closed quantities/units/dimensions (AFP/Wolfram Quantity discipline, absolute-vs-difference splits); claim/evidence types keeping measurement, numerical, parameter, and structural uncertainty distinct; the verdict×disposition taxonomy; the receipt format (source/IR/artifact hashes, solver identity+version, seeds, tolerances, replay instructions) with PROV-O/RO-Crate export; small golden corpora in each domain proving the IR can state (not solve) representative claims. Implemented depth — one vertical pair on the mathematics substrate: chemistry⇄biology reaction networks. Concretely: exact logical inference with proof objects over the KR core; native decidable proofs of stoichiometric consistency, atom/mass balance, and conservation (moiety) properties; vocabulary bound to ChEBI/GO (imported, not curated); SBML/SED-ML/OMEX import and export; kinetics delegated to at least two pinned external SBML-capable engines (e.g. COPASI, Tellurium/roadrunner) with BioSimulators-style cross-engine comparison feeding the verdict axis; batch obligations emitted as CWL; typed parameter evidence with uncertainty attached to rate constants and dose-response claims. Workflows: author → deterministic fail-closed compile → interactive logical queries (seconds) → batch simulation obligations → receipt → replay. Explicitly OUT of v1: physics beyond the units/frames type machinery (no PDEs, fields, continuum mechanics, hybrid-systems proving — all return `unsupported`); no in-house CAS or symbolic-integration engine (delegate, treat as oracle, re-check where decidable); no curated global knowledgebase (bind to registries); no parameter estimation/fitting; no stochastic simulation beyond what a delegated engine natively supports; no hosted service; no GUI beyond a minimal REPL/report surface.

## value_analysis

The chemistry⇄biology reaction-network pair generates the most value per unit of co-residence because it is the one workflow where every differentiator fires on a single artifact: one typed reaction statement (species from ChEBI, stoichiometry, rates with units, provenance) is simultaneously the object of exact proof (conservation and consistency are decidable linear/integer checks — cheap, native, certifiable), of delegated numeric simulation (pinned SBML engines, cross-engine verification), and of typed empirical evidence (measured rate constants with distinct uncertainty kinds). No other pair does this: math+physics puts the product head-to-head with the two best-resourced incumbents (Wolfram; MTK/JuliaHub — whose 2025-12-06 v11 split moving advanced compiler passes behind a commercial license is itself a trust-gap opening, but for v2, against entrenched competition) in a market that buys speed, not audit. The bio/chem side has the documented, institutionalized pain the product answers: 50% irreproducibility, units advisory by standard, no inference layer anywhere in the COMBINE stack, BioModels' curation bottleneck — and mature interop standards that let a solo effort ship into an existing ecosystem instead of beside it. What is lost by deferring physics depth: the largest modeling market, hybrid-systems verification (KeYmaera's turf), continuum/spatial modeling, and — the real cost — early stress-testing of reference frames and field quantities in the IR. What is gained: a beachhead where "auditable and replayable" is the buying criterion, adoption through existing standards rather than migration, and a differentiator set (proofs + receipts + typed uncertainty) that no incumbent in that ecosystem possesses even partially.

## weakest_point_of_own_case

The "four-domain IR" is unfalsified by construction in v1: only one pair exercises it, so the claim that the representation is domain-generic rests on spec-time judgment, not shipped evidence — and the survey documents that widening-after-shipping is exactly where representations break (SBML's Arrays package stalled as drafts for years; OWL fractured into profiles when tractability met reality; physics will demand reference frames, tensor/field quantities, and coordinate conventions the reaction-network vertical never stresses, risking breaking IR changes in v2). The counter-debater can fairly say every narrowed system also once had a general representation on paper, which makes "four-domain IR, one-pair implementation" a design intention wearing a spec — dangerously close to the presenting-aspirations-as-shipped sin the product's own doctrine forbids, unless the three thin domains' golden corpora are genuinely adversarial rather than ceremonial. Compounding this, the solo+12-month budget means the assurance machinery that makes fail-closed credible (differential oracles, conformance corpora, receipt replay) competes for the same year as the features it gates; and the LLM-demand thesis — that verifiable computation finds institutional buyers — is a market hypothesis with no shipped counterexample in the survey either way.
