## thesis

Ship v1 as an auditable compiler + orchestrator + receipts engine for biochemical reaction networks: chemistry and biology co-resident on a deliberately thin mathematical substrate, with physics and general mathematics explicitly out — because that pair is the only one where every differentiator (fail-closed units, verdicts with proof objects, typed uncertainty, replay receipts) answers a documented, quantified unmet need, where the solver obligations are tractable for one developer in twelve months, and where no incumbent occupies the ground.

## argument

Steelman the broad case first, because it is genuinely strong. The product's load-bearing layers — units, uncertainty kinds, verdict/disposition taxonomy, receipts — are domain-generic; building them once and amortizing across four sciences is the whole economic logic. The survey supports widening: Wolfram, Lean, and MTK broadened monotonically and thrived, while narrowers stalled (SBML's packages froze in draft; PROV froze entirely). Vocabulary breadth is a network effect (Wolfram's Entity framework). AI agents shift the labor curve exactly where breadth is expensive — corpus curation, backend bindings. And breadth is an architectural forcing function: a kernel that never faced reference frames or PDEs will bake in reaction-network assumptions, the OpenMath lesson that a term calculus designed for one interchange problem could not later carry semantics.

The rebuttal is that every widening survivor had what this project does not. Wolfram has hundreds of staff and still ships no provenance receipts and no auditable algorithms (its own docs concede Reduce is ~1400 pages of closed C). Lean has an FRO. MTK has a company — and the December 2025 v11 license split, moving the advanced compiler passes out of MIT, is direct evidence that the broad scientific compiler is so expensive it broke SciML's open model under commercial pressure. Meanwhile the "failed narrowers" are adoption successes read correctly: CWL's contraction into bioinformatics is where it actually got used; KeYmaera X deliberately dropped DAEs and quantified dL to keep a ~2,000-line kernel sound. Sage — the closest architectural cousin, one typed surface over ninety backends — is Stein's own verdict of "overall failed" against a four-system mission, with the specific failure being uncontrolled semantic divergence between backends (branch cuts, simplification domains). Breadth did not defeat that; per-seam judgment would have, and judgment is the one input agents do not multiply. On a solo/12-month budget, every added domain is subtracted assurance, and assurance is this product's only differentiator. A broad shallow v1 is a worse Wolfram; a narrow deep v1 is a new thing.

Why chemistry+biology over the other pairs. Math+physics is the maximal-incumbent pair: it means rebuilding what Modelica and MTK spent decades on — DAE index reduction and tearing, which the MTK digest explicitly warns not to underestimate — to reach users those tools already serve, and even the proof-carrying corner is claimed by KeYmaera X, which found it necessary to retreat to polynomial term languages. Physics also drags in reference frames and PDEs, the two most expensive KR features on the list. Chemistry+biology, instantiated as biochemical reaction networks, inverts every one of those liabilities. The co-residence value is concrete: chemistry supplies compile-time invariants (ChEBI-bound species identity, stoichiometric mass/charge balance, thermodynamic consistency of cycles) that must reside in the same compiler as biology's models and evidence to act as checks rather than conventions. Today that seam is exactly where the ecosystem fails silently: SBML makes units optional and advisory — unit-consistency violations are warnings — and BioModels' curators found roughly half of published models irreproducible. Split the pair into two systems and a non-conserving reaction or a mis-united rate constant type-checks, as it does now.

Every differentiator lands on a documented gap: no inference layer or verdict taxonomy anywhere in SBML/COPASI/PySB; point-estimate parameters with no typed uncertainty; advisory provenance. And every fix has a proven pattern to copy rather than invent: Antimony's readable-surface-to-standard-IR compilation, OMEX's one-archive receipt shape, KiSAO's solver-identity-and-tolerance ontology, BioSimulators' pinned containerized solvers with cross-engine comparison — a working prototype of reproducibility receipts. The solver obligation is stiff-ODE integration and parameter estimation delegated to existing engines (CVODE-class, COPASI-class): orchestration, not solver authorship. No index reduction, no PDEs, no frames. The mixed latency model maps cleanly — compilation, unit/conservation entailments, and structural verdicts are interactive; simulation and fitting are batch. The audience, though small, is the one community with a governed-vocabulary culture (OBO identifier discipline), regulatory pressure toward auditability (QSP models in submissions), and a quantified reproducibility wound — i.e., the population most likely to pay the fail-closed tax that scientists elsewhere refuse.

What is lost is real and should be stated without flinching: the entire Modelica/MTK audience; the headline four-domain demo; cross-domain queries (pharmacokinetics against physics-derived bounds); general symbolic mathematics — no integration, no equation solving beyond the network fragment, nothing for Lean/mathlib users; stochastic and spatial regimes deferred, disclosed honestly via out_of_regime rather than simulated badly. The addressable market shrinks from "computational science" to systems biology and pharmacometrics. That is the price of shipping receipts that are actually load-bearing in year one — and the layered-standards lesson from Modelica/FMI is that the narrow, minimal, typed seam (FMI: 280+ adopters) outlives the broad language (a dozen implementations).

## recommended_v1_scope

IN — Domains: chemistry (species identity bound to ChEBI-style governed registry; stoichiometry; mass/charge balance; thermodynamic-consistency checks on cycles) + biology (reaction networks/pathways, deterministic kinetic models, dose-response and perturbation evidence). Mathematical substrate only as needed: fail-closed Quantity-style units/dimensions, mass-action/polynomial rate laws, linear conservation-law analysis, Jacobian generation. IN — Workflows: (1) author models in the typed KR surface, deterministic compile to a solver-neutral IR with SBML import/export as the ecosystem on-ramp; (2) interactive logical tier: unit/dimension checking, conservation and structural entailments with proof objects, verdicts proved/disproved/unknown; (3) batch tier: stiff-ODE simulation and parameter estimation dispatched to at least two pinned, containerized external backends with cross-engine trajectory comparison (the BioSimulators mechanism) and the full disposition taxonomy; (4) evidence tier: typed empirical claims with measurement/numerical/parameter/structural uncertainty kept distinct, supported/refuted/conflicting verdicts against simulations, out_of_regime for mass-action validity limits (e.g., low copy number); (5) receipts on every result: OMEX-compatible archive + source/IR/artifact hashes + solver identity/version + seeds/tolerances + replay instructions, exportable to PROV-O/RO-Crate at the boundary. Distribution: linkable Rust library + self-hosted service only. OUT — explicitly: the physics domain (no mechanics, EM, thermal DAEs, no reference frames), PDEs and spatial fields, stochastic simulation (taxonomy-disclosed, not shipped), general computer algebra (no symbolic integration/general equation solving), general theorem proving, open ad-hoc vocabulary minting (extension only through the governed registry mechanism), and the hosted service.

## value_analysis

The pair generating the most co-residence value is chemistry+biology as biochemical reaction networks. The value is that chemistry-side invariants (species identity, stoichiometric conservation, thermodynamic consistency) only function as compile-time checks on biology-side models and evidence if both live in one compiler; separated, they degrade to conventions — which is the current, documented failure mode (SBML units advisory; ~50% of published models irreproducible per BioModels curation). Math+physics has high intrinsic co-residence value but near-zero marginal value: Modelica/MTK already capture it, at a compiler-pass cost (index reduction, tearing) that is decades-deep and explicitly warned against. Gained by the recommended scope: every differentiator (fail-closed units, verdicts, typed uncertainty, receipts) is load-bearing on day one in a community with quantified pain, governed-vocabulary culture, and copyable precedents (Antimony, OMEX, KiSAO, BioSimulators); solver obligations reduce to orchestrating existing stiff-ODE engines. Lost: the entire physics/engineering audience and the Modelica/MTK-adjacent market; general symbolic math and theorem-proving users; cross-domain queries spanning physics and biology; stochastic/spatial regimes (disclosed via out_of_regime rather than served); the four-domain headline demo. Net: the scope trades market breadth for the only property no incumbent has — auditable verdicts with replayable receipts — in the one domain that has already proven it wants them.

## weakest_point_of_own_case

Two connected weaknesses. First, the market: systems biology/pharmacometrics is small, its tooling is grant-funded and free (COPASI, Tellurium), and the community most tolerant of the fail-closed tax is also the least able to pay for it — so the narrow scope may prove product-market fit for adoption but not for sustainability, and a permissively licensed solo project has no moat if the mechanisms are validated and then absorbed by incumbents. Second, the pair is arguably one domain wearing two hats: chemistry enters mostly as constraint-supplier (stoichiometry, thermodynamic checks) rather than as a full co-equal domain, so the scope never truly stress-tests cross-domain co-residence — the architectural forcing-function objection (OpenMath's kernel could not later carry what it never faced) is deferred, not refuted, and the IR may bake in reaction-network assumptions that make the eventual physics extension a rewrite rather than an addition. Relatedly, the genuinely novel layer — typed evidence semantics with four distinct uncertainty kinds feeding supported/refuted/conflicting verdicts — has no shipped precedent anywhere in the survey, and my scope keeps that highest-risk component on the v1 critical path.
