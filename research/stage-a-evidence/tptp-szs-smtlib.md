## system

The automated-reasoning solver-interchange programme: TPTP problem/solution libraries + the SZS result ontology, SMT-LIB (with Z3/cvc5 as flagship implementations), the CASC and SMT-COMP competitions, and checkable certificate formats (DRAT, LFSC, Alethe, plus cvc5's CPC and the carcara checker)

## overlap

This ecosystem already ships, in production form, the product's two most distinctive plumbing claims. (1) Two-axis result taxonomy: the SZS ontology (tptp.org/UserDocs/SZSOntology/) separates a semantic *success* sub-ontology (Theorem, CounterSatisfiable, Satisfiable, Unsatisfiable, ContradictoryAxioms, Equivalent, EquiSatisfiable, Unknown — organized by isa-hierarchy over model relationships between axioms and conjecture) from a *no-success* sub-ontology explaining why no semantic value was established (Timeout, MemoryOut, ResourceOut, GaveUp, Error, Incomplete), reported machine-parsably as "% SZS status X for problem" with delimited "% SZS output start/end" solution blocks. That is the verdict/disposition split, operational since the 2000s across every CASC entrant. (2) Fail-closed typed surface: SMT-LIB is a sorted language where a symbol must be introduced by declare-sort/declare-fun under an explicitly declared logic before use; check-sat answers sat/unsat/unknown and (get-info :reason-unknown) returns a structured reason (memout | incomplete | s-expr) — verdict plus disposition again. (3) Untrusted-solver orchestration with independent checking: solvers emit unsat cores and proofs checked by separate tools — DRAT/drat-trim in SAT (clausal add/delete steps validated by unit propagation), LFSC and Alethe for SMT, carcara (Rust, Apache-2.0) as an Alethe checker/elaborator, GDV structurally verifying CASC proofs (leaves = problem formulae, root = conjecture or $false). (4) Honesty machinery: CASC soundness-tests every system on planted non-theorems and retrospectively disqualifies unsound systems; SMT-COMP 2025 rules define "erroneous" per track, validate unsat cores by majority vote of independent solvers, validate models with an independent tool (Dolmen), treat two sound solvers disagreeing on an unknown-status benchmark as an alarm (benchmark removed, disagreement published), scramble benchmarks so solvers cannot recognize inputs, and derive the competition seed from a publicly committed future value (NYSE Composite opening). This is exactly the proposed orchestrator's exact-logic core, its result taxonomy, its certificate seam, and its multi-solver honesty protocol.

## insufficiency

Concrete missing mechanisms, not ambitions: (1) No quantity semantics — SMT-LIB sorts are logical sorts over fixed theories (Ints, Reals, bit-vectors, FP, strings); there is no unit type, no dimensional-analysis check, no reference frame, so "missing units are compile errors" has no counterpart; a joule and a newton-metre-per-radian are both Real. (2) No empirical-claim layer — every input is an axiom with certainty 1; there is no typed evidence, no measurement/parameter/structural uncertainty distinction, hence no supported/refuted/conflicting verdicts; SZS/SMT-LIB verdicts are entailment-only and out_of_regime does not exist (regime is handled only statically, as set-logic violations). (3) No numerical computation or simulation — these solvers decide formulas; ODE/PDE integration, tolerances, non_convergent and numerically_indeterminate dispositions have no counterpart in SZS or SMT-LIB. (4) No provenance receipts in the formats themselves — SZS blocks carry proof text but no source/IR/artifact hashes, no solver-version binding inside the result, no replay instructions; the reproducibility machinery (BenchExec clusters, scramblers, archived binaries, committed seeds) lives in competition infrastructure, not in the interchange standard a user could link against. (5) No converged certificate seam — get-proof output remains solver-defined; DRAT covers only propositional clausal proofs; LFSC/Alethe/CPC fragment per solver; the SMT-COMP proof track explicitly declined to define a valid proof and vanished after 2023. (6) No human-readable KR surface — TPTP's Prolog-like syntax and SMT-LIB's S-expressions are machine-interchange formats with positional arguments and no named places, glossary, or domain vocabulary. (7) No orchestrator product — SystemOnTPTP is a remote batch service; there is no permissively-licensed linkable library that compiles a model, dispatches typed obligations to heterogeneous solvers, and assembles receipted results.

## narrowing_history

The logic coverage broadened rather than narrowed: TPTP grew from untyped FOF to typed TFF, arithmetic TFA, higher-order THF, and non-classical extensions, and SZS grew extra sub-ontologies (type-checking success, verification success, dataforms); CASC-30 (2025) runs ten divisions and SMT-LIB reached v2.7 (2026-03-27). The genuine narrowing is on the certificate axis. SMT proof standardization was an explicit community ambition for over a decade — Alethe's 2021 paper opens by noting veriT's proof format "was presented ten years ago at the first PxTP workshop" and pitches it as "towards a generic SMT proof format" seeking community feedback — yet the SMT-LIB standard still leaves proof output solver-defined, and Alethe's own specification is titled "An Evolving Specification and Reference." SMT-COMP institutionalized the retreat: the 2022 Proof Exhibition Track stated it was "non-competitive and we are not fixing the definition of a valid proof," accepted "holey" (incomplete) proofs as a checker outcome, ran again in 2023, and is absent from the 2024 and 2025 track lists and the 2025 rules (zero mentions of proofs, Alethe, LFSC, or DRAT — verified against the fetched rules PDF). The competitions therefore fell back to cheaper honesty mechanisms — cross-solver voting, model validation, disagreement reporting — instead of universal kernel-checked certificates. By contrast DRAT converged in SAT because propositional clausal proofs need one rule family plus deletion, no theories, no preprocessing semantics. Evidence basis: fetched track pages and rules; the interpretation that the proof track's absence is deliberate abandonment rather than pause is inference (no organizer statement fetched).

## lessons

Copy: (1) adopt the SZS shape rather than inventing the verdict/disposition split — use a closed semantic-success enum crossed with a closed no-success enum, machine-parsable one-line status plus delimited output blocks; (2) adopt SMT-LIB's discipline of declaring the regime up front (set-logic as ancestor of out_of_regime fail-closed at compile time) and fail-closed sorted declarations; (3) make disagreement the working oracle: route each obligation to ≥2 independent solvers where possible, treat divergence as an alarm and publish it, validate unsat-core-like artifacts by independent-solver vote and models by an independent validator, and soundness-test backends with planted known-status obligations, with retrospective invalidation of results from a backend later found unsound; (4) commit seeds publicly and scramble/canonicalize inputs so a backend cannot special-case them — the receipts design (seeds, versions, exact binaries) is precisely what SMT-COMP had to build around the standard because results vary across versions and seeds; (5) separate solver from checker as paired artifacts, and support proof *elaboration* (carcara's model) to bridge coarse solver output to strict checking. Mistake to avoid: do not gate the product on a universal certificate format. Twenty years of fragmentation (veriT/Alethe, LFSC, cvc5's CPC, Z3's own format), an "evolving" Alethe spec, and a competition proof track that refused to define a valid proof and then disappeared show the realistic seam is per-backend certificate formats with the receipt recording certificate format + checker identity + checker verdict (including "holey"), not one mandated format. Secondary lesson: keep disposition enums closed — Z3 shipped non-compliant :reason-unknown responses when the standard left room for free-form s-expressions (Z3 issue #734).

## cluster_answer

Mapping. SZS success values → verdict axis: Theorem→proved; CounterSatisfiable (and Unsatisfiable of the conjecture)→disproved; Satisfiable→a model-exists verdict the product folds under proved/supported for consistency claims; Unknown→unknown; ContradictoryAxioms→the product's invalid_model — instructively, SZS classifies it as a *success* (a semantic determination about the input), suggesting the product should treat "model provably inconsistent" as a verdict, not a disposition. SZS has nothing mapping to supported/refuted/conflicting/out_of_regime — those require the empirical layer the ecosystem lacks. SZS no-success → disposition axis: Timeout/MemoryOut/ResourceOut→resource_exhausted; Error (Syntax/Usage/Input)→invalid_input; Inappropriate/NotTried→unsupported; GaveUp/Incomplete→a disposition the product's list lacks ("procedure incomplete, gave up honestly") — worth adding rather than overloading unknown. backend_unavailable has no SZS counterpart (SZS assumes the system ran); non_convergent/numerically_indeterminate have none (logic-only world). SMT-LIB: sat/unsat/unknown→verdict (with polarity inversion: unsat of the negated goal = proved); (get-info :reason-unknown) with memout|incomplete|s-expr→disposition; set-logic violations are fail-closed compile errors — the static ancestor of out_of_regime. What the competitions learned about honesty: CASC pre-tests all systems on planted non-theorems, requires SZS status + delimited proof output with no interleaved noise, syntax-checks proofs (tptp4X) and structurally verifies them (GDV: problem formulae as leaves, conjecture or $false as root), and retrospectively disqualifies systems whose unsoundness cannot be ruled out. SMT-COMP defines "erroneous" per track, computes soundness against known statuses, removes unknown-status benchmarks when two otherwise-sound solvers disagree while publishing the disagreement, validates unsat cores by majority vote of independent checking solvers ("no checking solver produces unsat → the core is not validated"), validates models with the independent Dolmen tool, scrambles benchmarks, and derives its randomness from the publicly committed NYSE Composite opening value — seed/version sensitivity is so real the competition freezes solver versions on dated deadlines and archives exact binaries. Why certificates failed to converge: SMT proofs must encode heterogeneous theory reasoning plus aggressive preprocessing, so each format mirrors one solver's internal calculus (cvc5's CPC "faithfully reflects its reasoning"; Alethe grew out of veriT); a common format forces either holes (the 2022 track's accepted "holey" verdict) or costly elaboration; the SMT-LIB standard deliberately leaves get-proof output solver-defined; and the proof-exhibition track was explicitly non-competitive, never fixed a proof definition, and was dropped after 2023. DRAT converged in SAT only because clausal proofs need a single rule family with deletion and no theories. Net effect on the survey's conclusion: correct — the product should adopt/extend the SZS+SMT-LIB shapes (adding the empirical and numerical verdicts/dispositions they lack) and design its certificate seam per-backend from day one, inheriting the fragmentation as a fact rather than an obstacle to fix.
