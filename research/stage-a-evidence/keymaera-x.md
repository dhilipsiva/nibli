## system

KeYmaera X (LS-Lab/KeYmaeraX-release) — an axiomatic tactical theorem prover for hybrid systems, implementing differential dynamic logic (dL) and differential game logic (dGL), from André Platzer's Logical Systems Lab (CMU, now KIT). Written in Scala on the JVM; latest release 5.1.2 (2025-07-03), repo active into 2026.

## overlap

KeYmaera X is the closest working instance of the proposed product's "exact logical inference with proof objects over physical models" core, restricted to hybrid (discrete+continuous ODE) dynamics. Mechanism-level overlap: (1) A typed declarative modeling language — .kyx archive files declare hybrid programs (assignments, tests, nondeterministic choice/loops, ODE systems x'=f(x) & Q) plus dL formulas [α]φ / ⟨α⟩φ; since 5.0 it has user-defined function/predicate definition packages, i.e. declared vocabulary that the parser checks rather than defaults. (2) A small trusted soundness kernel: ~1,700–2,000 lines of soundness-critical code, LCF-style — every proof, however produced (automated tactics, invariant generators like Pegasus, user scripts), must pass through the kernel, so the answer is a checkable derivation, not a search transcript. This is the same "every conclusion is a formal derivation" stance the product wants, achieved by uniform substitution: axioms are concrete object-level formulas and one substitution rule instantiates them, eliminating schema side conditions from trusted code (Platzer, JAR 2017, relatively complete calculus). (3) Solver orchestration with a declared trust boundary: real-arithmetic goals are closed by external quantified-elimination backends — Wolfram Mathematica/Wolfram Engine or bundled Z3 — invoked as trusted oracles, exactly analogous to the product's "compute-backend results are trusted as axioms" and its backend_unavailable disposition. (4) Layered untrusted automation: the Bellerophon tactic language plus proof-search sit outside the kernel, mirroring the product's plan of deterministic compilation plus delegated heavy obligations. (5) Downstream artifacts resembling "receipts": stored proof terms/lemmas in archive files are re-checkable; ModelPlex synthesizes provably correct runtime monitors from proofs, and VeriPhy (research pipeline) carries verified models toward executable controllers. (6) Real case studies in the product's ambition space: ACAS X airborne collision avoidance (FAA context), train control, ground robots — physics-plus-control claims proved, not simulated.

## insufficiency

Concrete missing mechanisms relative to the proposed product: (a) Domain scope — the term language is real-arithmetic (polynomial/rational); transcendental and special functions are not first-class (only differentially-definable encodings; approximate axiomatization of such functions is still a 2025-era research paper, arXiv 2506.08233). No PDEs, no spatial fields, no stochastic dynamics in the shipped tool, no chemistry/biology ontologies — it cannot state reaction networks with units, dose-response data, or frame-dependent quantities as such. (b) No unit or dimension system: variables are dimensionless reals; a third-party fork (vincom2/KeYmaeraX-release units-of-measure branch) exists precisely because the mainline lacks it — the product's "missing units are compile errors" has no counterpart. (c) No empirical-evidence layer: no typed measurement claims, no uncertainty taxonomy, no supported/refuted/conflicting verdicts — dL verdicts are proved/not-proved against an assumed-exact model; measurement, parameter and structural uncertainty are outside the logic (robustness must be hand-encoded as interval nondeterminism). (d) No numerical simulation orchestration: backends are QE/arithmetic oracles, not ODE integrators or FEM solvers; there are no tolerances, seeds, or convergence dispositions. (e) Reproducibility receipts are informal: proofs are re-checkable and releases are Zenodo-DOI'd, but there is no hashed source→IR→artifact chain binding solver identity/version into a portable envelope. (f) Distribution/embedding: JVM/Scala under GPL-2.0 — not linkable from a Rust product under MIT/Apache-2.0, and not a WASM-able library. (g) The arithmetic oracle (Mathematica) is proprietary and in the TCB; the verified-kernel work (Isabelle/Coq, CPP 2017) covers the calculus, not the QE backend.

## narrowing_history

It narrowed deliberately, and the narrowing is documented by the maintainers. The predecessor KeYmaera (2007–2015, built as a hybrid extension of the KeY Java verifier) supported more: differential-algebraic equations and differential inequalities, quantified dL (QdL) for distributed hybrid systems (KeYmaera 3.0+), and differential temporal properties. KeYmaera X (CADE-25, 2015) was a clean-slate rewrite that traded that breadth for a minimal kernel: soundness-critical code shrank from 65,989 lines (KeYmaera) to ~1,700 lines (both figures stated on symbolaris.com/info/KeYmaera.html, which marks KeYmaera as outdated and superseded). Distributed hybrid systems and differential temporal properties were explicitly NOT carried over. The driver was soundness assurance: KeYmaera inherited KeY's large schema-based rule engine, where a single mis-specified side condition is unsound; uniform substitution made a 3-orders-of-magnitude smaller trusted core possible, later cross-verified in Isabelle/HOL and Coq (Bohrer et al., CPP 2017). Scope has since been re-widened only where the kernel discipline permits (differential game logic via uniform substitution, ODE invariant generation via Pegasus). So the ambition that narrowed was feature breadth of the logic implementation, not the CPS mission; the lesson is that they paid for trustworthiness with expressiveness and then re-earned expressiveness axiom by axiom.

## adoption

Honest reading: a healthy, actively maintained academic tool with deep but narrow adoption — the research community standard for hybrid-systems theorem proving, not an industry-deployed product. Observable evidence (2026-08-26): 101 stars, 49 forks, 16,962 commits, 15 open issues; releases 5.0 (2022-08-11) → 5.1.2 (2025-07-03) via GitHub API; search-result metadata shows repo updates as recent as May 2026. Flagship applications are peer-reviewed case studies rather than production deployments: ACAS X collision-avoidance verification (Springer 2015; hybrid-games extension in ACM TECS 2022) fed analysis back to the real FAA-run system but KeYmaera X is not itself in the certification loop. It anchors CMU's Foundations of CPS course and Platzer's LFCPS textbook, the Pegasus invariant generator (FM 2019 best tool paper), ModelPlex, and VeriPhy. No package-registry footprint to measure; user base is plausibly hundreds of researchers/students, not thousands of engineers.

## lessons

Copy: (1) the LCF discipline scaled to continuous math — a ~2,000-line kernel where uniform substitution turns axiom schemas into data (concrete formulas) plus ONE trusted rule, so adding reasoning power (dGL, new ODE axioms, Pegasus) never grows the TCB; the product's compile-to-FOL/typed-IR core should have the same property, with automation and solver orchestration strictly outside the kernel. (2) Declared, checked trust boundaries for external solvers (QE oracle = axiom source, disposition when absent), and proof objects re-checkable without the search that found them; cross-verify the kernel in an independent system (Isabelle/Coq) as they did. (3) Ship monitors/artifacts derived FROM proofs (ModelPlex) — a proof that leaves the tool as a deployable check is the adoption vector. Avoid: (1) KeYmaera's mistake, the one KeYmaera X exists to fix — a large schema/side-condition rule engine (66k soundness-critical lines) accreted by features; do not let the product's compiler grow soundness-critical special cases per scientific domain. (2) Putting a proprietary solver (Mathematica) in the TCB with no receipt binding its identity/version — the product's reproducibility envelope is precisely the missing mechanism. (3) GPL + JVM as distribution: it forecloses embedding, which caps adoption at "tool people visit" rather than "library people ship". (4) Expect the narrowness: a decade in, units of measure still live in an abandoned third-party fork — first-class units/frames/uncertainty must be in the core type system from day one or they never arrive.

## cluster_answer

What makes it work: three interlocking choices. dL gives a compositional logic where hybrid programs (discrete assignments/tests/loops + ODEs with evolution domains) are first-class modalities, so "the controller keeps the plant safe" is one formula [α*]φ, and differential invariants/ghosts let ODE properties be proved axiomatically without closed-form solutions. Uniform substitution (Platzer, JAR 59(2), 2017 — sound and relatively complete) makes axioms concrete formulas instantiated by a single substitution rule, collapsing the trusted base to ~1,700–2,000 lines of Scala, later cross-verified in Isabelle/Coq (CPP 2017). Everything else — Bellerophon tactics, proof search, Pegasus invariant generation — is untrusted and must re-derive through the kernel. Actual scope limits: it can state only what compiles to polynomial/rational real arithmetic over ODEs — no PDEs (so no continuum mechanics, fields, transport), no stochastic dynamics in the tool, no first-class transcendental/special functions (differentially-definable encodings only; still active research in 2025), no units, frames, or measurement uncertainty; and every proof bottoms out in real QE, which is decidable but doubly exponential, so proofs of even 10–20-dimensional systems need human-supplied invariants and the QE oracle (Mathematica/Z3) sits unverified in the TCB. Adoption reality: the academic standard and a live project (5.1.2 in July 2025, commits into 2026, 101 stars), taught at CMU, with genuine external-facing wins (ACAS X analysis for an FAA-context system), but no production/industrial embedding and no ecosystem footprint. What it teaches about proving statements over continuous dynamics: you never need to solve the ODE — you need invariants, and invariant generation, not proof checking, is the hard open problem; exactness survives contact with physics only where the model is exact, so the verdict is always conditional on model assumptions (KeYmaera X leaves those assumptions implicit in the formula — the proposed product's typed assumptions/out_of_regime verdict is exactly the missing complement); and a minimal kernel plus substitution-as-data is how you keep a growing scientific logic sound with a solo-scale team.
