## assessment

The surveyed set is strong and largely sufficient on five of the product's axes — equation-oriented modeling and solver seams (Modelica/FMI, MTK), symbolic breadth and curation (Wolfram, Sage), proof objects and trust architecture (Lean, Isabelle/Rocq, KeYmaera X), domain standards and replay (COMBINE, COPASI/BioModels, CWL, PROV/RO-Crate/Nix), and vocabulary governance (OBO/OWL, OpenMath) — and it already supports the central explanation that each capability exists separately while the integration does not. But it is blind on exactly the two axes the product claims as most novel, which currently makes the novelty conclusion look stronger than it is: no surveyed system addresses typed uncertainty/evidence (Stan and the V&V/UQ programme both partially solve it and teach hard-won failure modes), and no surveyed system supplies a shipped result taxonomy or many-solver certificate ecosystem (the SZS ontology and SMT-LIB already are a working verdict/disposition split with checkable certificates, so the product should be positioned as adopting, not inventing, that shape). A fourth, lesser gap is feasibility history: Cyc is the only real precedent for a small-team, hand-curated, cross-domain formal KB with inference, and its microtheory mechanism is the closest prior art for assumption-scoped validity. With those four clusters added, the survey set would be adequate to answer why this product does not already exist.
