## B.2 §3 The shared-semantics decision

Evidence: `stage-b-evidence/architecture.md` (the decision memo, with its precedent claims primary-sourced 2026-08-26); adopted here with rulings on its open questions.

### The decision: Shape B, with two anti-decay refinements `[proposal]`

Of the three candidate shapes — (A) one universal IR, (B) one shared semantic envelope containing typed domain IRs, (C) minimal common claim model + independent per-domain languages — **Shape B is adopted**. A fails either as OpenMath (prose semantics, serialization format) or as the fidelity test's named failure (graphs/tensors/coordinates as generic predicates), or by kernel accretion; C forfeits the compile-time co-residence checks that motivated the chem+bio pairing, loses cross-domain correlation structure (the decisive trace: the yeast slice's shared-T,P covariance must span physics and chemistry lowerings inside *one* propagation obligation), and rebuilds OpenMath's N² phrasebook burden. Two refinements prevent B's decay modes:

1. **The two-spine rule.** The envelope is the *claim spine* (claims, evidence, contexts, verdicts/dispositions, quantities, identifiers, equality). The mathematics IR is the *computational lowering substrate*: domain IRs form a typed import DAG (physics and chemistry import math; biology imports chemistry and math), and every numeric obligation payload is a math-IR expression with an explicit domain of definition, produced by a deterministic, citable lowering owned by the emitting domain IR. Cross-domain sharing happens through envelope types or declared DAG imports — never string conventions.
2. **The envelope admission rule.** A type enters the envelope only if (i) ≥2 domain IRs need it, (ii) it has an external-standard oracle (the transcription-only ruling), and (iii) its values cross domain boundaries *uninterpreted*. Quantities, uncertainty/covariance objects, identifiers, contexts, claim kinds, and equality pass; tensors, molecular graphs, frames, and genomic coordinates fail (iii) and stay domain-side. This rule is the control against envelope creep toward A — and it is honestly `[open]` whether such a rule holds over years; no shipped system has demonstrated one.

### The envelope inventory `[proposal]`

**Quantity** (the load-bearing envelope type): payload (exact rational | Float{prec, rnd} with §0.5 exactness class | interval/ball enclosure | distribution ref) × unit (UCUM-grammar expression over the B.4 unit kernel) × quantity-kind identifier × optional uncertainty ref (the B.7 budget-bearing type). Covariance/correlation structure is a first-class envelope object (shared-influence graph first; declared edges boundary-only, per B.7). **Identifiers**: CURIE `prefix:local-id` against a first-party committed prefix registry pinning per prefix the source registry, release version, license, and mechanizable oracle (Bioregistry-aligned where defined); claim/context/receipt ids are content-addressed digests using the GA4GH computed-identifier construction (base64url(sha512(blob)[:24]) over a canonical serialization). Units are not CURIEs — compositional expressions cannot be enumerated. **Equality — three relations, never one overloaded `=`**: exact/definitional (decidable per type, identity *layer* declared per type); `approx_within(tol, metric)` with tol a unit-checked Quantity and the metric named; enclosure containment (always proved-with-certificate per A.4). Plus one fail-closed rule adopted verbatim: **`=` between two measurement-kind claims is a compile error** — measurement comparison goes through a declared statistical criterion returning `supported`/`refuted`/`indeterminate{measurement, margin}`. **Assumptions attach at exactly three typed points**: theory scopes at contexts (B.8 lifting), validity regimes at models (A.5.4), side conditions at operations (discharge or first-class unevaluated form). **Time**: a minimal instant/interval type is admitted to the envelope (passes all three admission criteria; RFC 3339/ISO 8601 profile as oracle) with ordering and duration only — no calendar arithmetic in v1 `[proposal, ruling on the memo's open question]`.

### Obligation languages — the third species `[proposal]`

Neither envelope nor domain IR: a typed problem statement handed to a solver. Every obligation language declares eight things: input signature (envelope + domain types); **fragment** (the SMT-LIB `set-logic` analogue — constructs outside it are compile errors, the static ancestor of `out_of_regime`); method identity as a governed vocabulary term (KiSAO where it exists; first-party `zz`-style synthetic method ids elsewhere, following Nibli's synthetic-provenance pattern `[local-fact]`) with typed parameters (tolerances are Quantities, unit-checked); budget; **result subset** (which verdicts/dispositions it may return, with payload types — making "a solver returned something it cannot mean" a type error); determinism class + seed requirements; backend binding constraints (solver identity + version); certificate kind. Inventory: **eight designed four-wide** — `obl:logic`, `obl:exact-linear-algebra`, `obl:uncertainty-propagation`, `obl:ode-simulation`, `obl:stochastic-simulation` (seed mandatory), `obl:symbolic` (side conditions discharge or return typed-partial/unevaluated), `obl:optimization`, `obl:enclosure` (results proved-with-certificate) — **three built for the vertical**: logic, uncertainty-propagation, ode/stochastic-simulation. Obligations are solver-class-shaped, never domain-shaped: one ODE obligation serves chemistry kinetics and physics dynamics alike.

### Cross-domain morphisms and versioning `[proposal]`

All cross-domain identity bridging and lifting goes through a **committed, versioned morphism registry** (source type, target type, direction, losslessness, external transcription authority — e.g. UniProt cross-references for the enzyme-as-biology-entity ↔ chemistry-species bridge); a missing morphism is a compile error naming the pair; no heuristic pushout anywhere — the SageMath lesson hardened into fail-closed form. Domain rules that constrain envelope values (a rate constant's unit as a function of reaction order; a stoichiometric balance) compile to **typed envelope constraints** the envelope checker verifies without domain knowledge — the seam discipline that lets chemistry check biology without merging IRs. **Versioning: lockstep, no optional packages** — every release ships all four domain IR schemas against one envelope version; capability gaps are `unsupported` dispositions declared in the capability matrix, never schema optionality (the SBML stalled-draft and MTK churn lessons).

Rulings on the memo's remaining open questions: quantity-kind identifiers are QUDT IRIs where they exist, with a first-party transcribed kind list (ISO 80000-sourced) filling gaps — final vocabulary at Stage C `[open]`; `invalid_model` stays a disposition (Stage A `[open]` stands; obligation result-subsets will be restated if it moves); the domain-payload boundary (whether any obligation takes a domain object directly, e.g. future substructure search) is scoped: the two-spine lowering rule governs *numeric* obligations only, and a future `obl:graph-query` would take chemistry-IR payloads legitimately — recorded so B does not quietly forbid it.

*Confidence: high on the shape decision (five independent tests converge, each grounded in measured prior-art failures); medium on the obligation inventory's completeness (derived from precedent + the vertical, and B.3–B.6 may split entries); medium on the admission rule holding long-term. Largest unverified assumption (inherited from the memo, and the same surface as B.7's trigger): that quantities/uncertainty/correlation can live wholly in the envelope with domain IRs only emitting typed constraints — if a domain must interpret uncertainty inside its own semantics (stochastic rate laws where a parameter's distribution is model content, not measurement metadata), the envelope/domain split leaks; B.7's exhibition covers the yeast slice but not that case, which is registered as a fidelity-battery target (B.9).*

## B.3 §4 Mathematics — the substrate IR

Evidence: `stage-b-evidence/math.md` (every version/license/status claim primary-sourced 2026-08-26). All recommendations adopted; the one owner ruling is queued for Gate 1.

**The number tower `[proposal]`:** ℤ and ℚ as arbitrary-precision ground kinds with exact structural equality (default backends permissive pure-Rust — num-bigint/dashu; GMP/MPFR/FLINT behind an off-by-default cargo feature, because statically-linked LGPL burdens every downstream of an MIT OR Apache-2.0 library). Algebraic numbers canonically as the **qqbar model** — unique reduced minimal polynomial over ℤ plus certified isolating ball, decidable equality, the (minpoly, box) pair doubling as an independently checkable certificate; radical towers are surface syntax only (non-canonical, dead past degree 4). **Float{prec, rnd} is a distinct kind from ℚ** with MPFR semantics normative: per-variable precision, per-operation rounding mode, correct rounding as the contract, *no global numeric state anywhere* — every emitted digit a typed, reproducible claim. **Finite fields ℤ/pℤ, F_q are first-class**, motivated as certificate machinery for the stoichiometric core: Freivalds O(n²) verification, Dumas–Kaltofen interactive certificates for determinant/rank/minpoly/PSD, CRT/Dixon-lifted exact solving with residue re-checks. **Two enclosure kinds**: IEEE 1788.1-2017 inf-sup intervals with decorations (via inari, MIT; 1788-2015 went Inactive-Reserved 2026-03-26 — frozen text, which *suits* transcription-only governance) and Arb-style mid-rad balls (feature-gated) for arbitrary precision; both are proved-with-certificate carriers; the decoration system maps onto typed partiality and `out_of_regime` (mapping ownership `[open]`). Nibli's `Number(f64)` with bitwise store equality and tolerant compute equality is **explicitly not inherited** — its own GUARANTEES document why `[local-fact]`.

**The symbolic layer `[proposal]`:** a small typed term calculus — application, *binding as first-class syntax*, attribution — transcribing the OpenMath object *mechanism* (never its dormant CDs). Derivatives, integrals, sums, limits are binder-operators whose **unevaluated form is a first-class value** and whose evaluation is a separate typed judgment carrying regime (differentiability class, integrability) — never silent. Equations/inequalities are first-class relational objects. Matrices/tensors are shape-indexed types over decidable linear-integer index arithmetic — the *same* machinery as unit-exponent vectors; **full dependent types are not required by any primary workflow**. Simplification is e-graph equality saturation (egg, MIT) with every rewrite a named cited axiom and the extraction trace a replayable certificate; guard-conditioned rewrites (x/x→1 needs x≠0) are enforced typed partiality. Constraint solving emits SMT-LIB2; solver answers upgrade to `proved` only with a checkable certificate (Alethe→Carcara, LRAT), else `supported` with solver identity recorded.

**The FOL boundary, named as the prompt demands:** all five primary workflows are FOL-or-below (stoichiometry = computation + certificates; unit algebra = a decidable equational theory; propagation = matrix computation; inference = Datalog/FOL; regimes = SMT-decidable guards). The first competency question that genuinely requires more: *"every left null vector w of stoichiometric matrix N yields a conserved quantity wᵀx(t) for all solutions of the induced ODE"* — quantification over the solution function space. Ruling: the kernel *states* such claims as typed theorem-statement objects and discharges them **only by reference to external proof artifacts** (Lean statement export; leanchecker/lean4lean re-check), never by in-kernel HO proof search.

**Queued owner ruling (Gate 1):** may a Monte-Carlo-verified certificate (Freivalds, interactive linear-algebra) carry `proved` with a recorded error bound below a declared threshold, or must it land as `supported{margin}`? Provisional architect position: a distinct certificate class `randomized{ε}`, mapping to `proved` only under an explicitly declared, context-visible threshold policy — the zero-silent-defaults stance applied to ε. `[open]`

*Confidence: high — versions, licenses, and standard statuses all primary-sourced; the FOL-boundary analysis is reasoned inference. Largest unverified assumption: that no permissive pure-Rust library delivers MPFR-grade correct rounding for transcendentals — this drives the feature-gated-LGPL architecture and is untested (flagged in the evidence file).*

## B.4 §5 Physics — the domain IR

Evidence: `stage-b-evidence/physics.md` (SI Brochure 9 read from its own V4.01 PDF; UCUM 2.2, QUDT, CODATA 2022 primary-sourced 2026-08-26). All recommendations adopted, including the boldest.

**The unit kernel `[proposal]`:** no single standard survives adoption verbatim — SI has no affine/log algebra and its own footnote calls rad = m/m "misleading"; UCUM forbids algebra on special units and demotes the mole; QUDT's DeciB carries conversionMultiplier 0.0 (a confession its machinery cannot compute logarithms). So the product writes its **own normative small-step algebra**, citing SI §2.3.3/§5.4.7 and UCUM §21–25 clause by clause, with each standard assigned one role: **SI = ratio-scale kernel and rendering; ISO 80000 = quantity-kind names (summaries until purchased, `[evidence-incomplete]`); UCUM = fail-closed I/O grammar; QUDT = identifier namespace only.** Structure: a dimension vector over SI's seven base dimensions **plus Angle as an eighth** (rad coherent; sr = rad²; SI-compliance restored by an explicit, cited *erasure lift* rad↦1 at declared boundaries — making torque≠energy and Hz≠rad/s type errors, with UCUM's radian-as-base-unit as deployed precedent), × **five scale classes as types with fixed operation tables**: Ratio (full algebra), Affine (point/difference split — a 5 °C difference is not 278.15 K; point+point is a compile error), Level (logarithmic: parameterized by base, power-vs-root-power convention, and a mandatory typed reference — a bare "dB" or "pH" without reference is a compile error; level+level only via named lowering operations), Count⟨E⟩ (entity-typed counts over governed vocabulary; mol stays a dimension with exact N_A as the sole Count↔amount bridge), Arbitrary (opaque imported assay units, mutually incomparable per UCUM §24–25), × a quantity-kind identifier (distinguishing torque from energy, Hz from rad/s at equal dimension).

**Constants `[proposal]`:** a pinned, versioned **evidence pack, never compiler builtins** — CODATA 2022 (RMP 97, 025002) with the seven exact defining constants as exact decimals, derived exacts (ħ = h/2π, R = N_A·k) as *symbolic expressions over exact constants* (ħ is irrational — "exact" must be allowed to mean "exact expression" or the store lies at some precision), and measured constants (G, α, masses) carrying GUM uncertainties and published correlations. Exactness is release-relative metadata (h was measured before 2019). CODATA 2026's deadline is 2026-12-31 — **the cache-invalidation path will be exercised in the product's first year by the world, not by a test**.

**Frames, tensors, spinors — honesty boundaries `[proposal]`:** v1–v2 frames are **typed frame tags + declared transformations** (cross-frame mixing without a declared transform is a compile error); the IR *defines* Frame as a fixed chart with a distinguished frame field, so the v3+ atlas generalization is additive; curved/no-global-frame cases return `unsupported` — never flattened (SageManifolds and mathlib evidence: full chart machinery is expert-years, with no vertical-slice payoff). Tensors are **abstract-index typed**: slots typed by (space, variance, declared symmetries); contraction is the total typed operation Gate 1 demands, defined exactly on same-space opposite-variance pairs; raising/lowering requires a declared metric; components exist only as frame-tagged lowerings for solvers; Young-projector canonicalization deferred to v2 (xAct/Cadabra named as the prior art). Spinors: typed representation indices over a declared Spin group, admissible on flat backgrounds or under a declared global tetrad — honest within that scope because the spin bundle is trivial there; nontrivial spin structures are `unsupported`.

**Regimes — one new physics rule `[proposal]`:** a validity-regime predicate whose evaluation requires the model's own output is **refused as circular** (the ideal-gas regime must not be |pV/nRT − 1| ≤ 0.02 — the model certifying itself); sound encodings are bounds on independent reduced variables citing a corresponding-states source, or reference to an imported compressibility correlation carrying its own uncertainty — in which case the regime check itself returns a margin and cites its basis. Regime predicates are sourced, versioned, owner-scoped claims.

*Confidence: high on units/constants (verified against the standards' own texts); medium on frame/tensor sequencing (cost evidence real, payoff judgment inferential). Largest unverified assumption: that Angle-as-eighth-dimension round-trips real SI-conformant corpora without a combinatorial burden of erasure lifts pushing authors to blanket erasure — no corpus-scale precedent exists; pre-registered as a B.9 battery target and a Stage E corpus test.*

## B.5 §6 Chemistry — the domain IR

Evidence: `stage-b-evidence/chemistry.md` (InChI Trust FAQ, O'Boyle 2012, RDKit/OpenBabel docs, RInChI paper, registry licenses — primary-sourced 2026-08-26). All recommendations adopted. The through-line: **every identity string in chemistry is a lossy projection of a typed molecular graph under a pinned normalization; the graph is the semantics, the strings are keys.**

**Identity `[proposal]`:** the IR-native **typed molecular graph is canonical** — atoms as (element, optional nuclide, formal charge, unpaired-electron field), explicit bond orders (Kekulé), explicit H counts, typed stereo descriptors; **no aromatic bond type exists in the IR**. Standard InChI + InChIKey, computed by the vendored IUPAC implementation pinned at an exact 1.07.x version (MIT since 1.07.1 — vendoring is license-clean), is the **species-class key**, computed *from* the graph, never the reverse; a version bump is a supersession event. Aromaticity gets the survey's most elegant ruling: **no normative perception model, by design** — aromatic-form SMILES is kekulized at import (kekulization failure = fail-closed error naming the delocalization subgraph); "is aromatic" exists only as a computed predicate *explicitly parameterized by a named model* (`aromatic(ring, model: rdkit_default | daylight | mdl)`) — a model-relative claim, never identity. A product that picks one model silently inherits its documented disagreements (azulene, 2-pyridone; RDKit's 24-atom cap; ~1.8% OpenBabel-vs-InChI stereo divergence); a product that stores Kekulé graphs and tags the model cannot be wrong about it.

**The ambiguous-identity contract (the §14 negative case, now derivable):** imports always recompute Standard InChI from the parsed graph with the pinned implementation; fail-closed triggers are (a) supplied ≠ recomputed, (b) one registry ID with two distinct recomputed InChIs across sources, (c) kekulization/stereo parse failure. The error payload names both strings, the **first differing InChI layer** (a /t diff means stereo perception; a /c diff means a different molecule), both provenances, and the pinned algorithm version — layer-granular, diagnosable, and never resolved by majority vote (resolution only by declared policy, per A.5.2/B.8).

**The type ladder `[proposal]`:** Element → Nuclide → AtomSite → MolecularGraph → **ChemicalSpecies** (graph fixed in tautomer *and* protonation state) → **TautomerClass** (the Standard-InChI mobile-H equivalence class — a distinct linked type; InChI's own standard-vs-fixed-H architecture proves species and class are different objects) → Substance/Mixture/Phase. **Species identity is context-free**; pH-dependence is a *speciation claim* in a context carrying pH/T/ionic strength, resolved by B.8 lifting — a query naming a class where a micro-species is required, without a determining context, fails closed. Reactions: **Reaction** (multiset of (species, exact rational coefficient), agents, direction) vs **ReactionInstance** (+ typed conditions and amounts); kinetic, equilibrium, and thermodynamic claims are three distinct claim kinds attached to them, never fields; RInChI is an export/lookup key only (its spec drops coefficients, maps, conditions). **Atom mapping is typed-partial evidence, never identity** — NP-hard, symmetry-degenerate, best tools 83–98% on golden sets; maps are provenance-mandatory claims, multiple maps coexist, reaction equality never reads them, and a mechanism query with no map in context fails closed.

**Registries and balance `[proposal]`:** bind ChEBI (CC BY 4.0, monthly pinned releases) and PubChem CID (unrestricted, retrieval-dated); **CAS is a boundary** — RNs pass through as opaque user annotations only, and CAS Common Chemistry (CC BY-NC) must never be vendored into an MIT OR Apache-2.0 + hosted product. Balance checking is exact rational **verification, never solving**: composition vectors over ℚ (element rows — or nuclide rows under a declared per-reaction isotope-tracking mode, never mixed — plus a mandatory charge row); the checker verifies the declared coefficient vector lies in the nullspace, sidestepping the dim>1 trap (composite reactions have infinitely many balancings — auto-balancing would be a hidden default). First-class corners: e⁻ as a species (half-reactions), hydrate dot-components, radicals (spin field; their InChI class key documented spin-blind); polymers/Markush/nonstoichiometric species are representable but balance-`unsupported` naming the offender. Unbalanced ⇒ `invalid_model` with per-row imbalance payload.

*Confidence: high on identity architecture and registry licensing (primary-sourced); medium on the balance corner inventory (design judgment where no IUPAC normative text exists). Largest unverified assumption: that the pinned InChI C library is deterministic and platform-stable enough to be the single identity oracle — cross-platform byte-identity of outputs was not verified; a same-graph-two-builds determinism differential is pre-registered for the Stage E corpus.*

## B.6 §7 Biology — the domain IR

Evidence: `stage-b-evidence/biology.md` (GA4GH VRS 2.0.1/refget/seqcol, NCBI/Ensembl/HGNC versioning, Cellosaurus, SO/OBI/GO — primary-sourced 2026-08-26). All recommendations adopted; one governance ruling made below.

**Identity and coordinates `[proposal]`:** the GA4GH digest stack is **adopted outright and computed in-house** — refget SQ digests as native Sequence identity, seqcol digests for reference sets (with its `/comparison` semantics as the compile-time "can these two coordinate-bearing claims share a context" check), VRS 2.0.1 computed identifiers for variants/locations, including VRS *normalization inside the compiler* so any two spellings of one variant compile to one digest; user-supplied ga4gh CURIEs are recomputed and verified, mismatch = compile error. The canonical coordinate type: `GenomicInterval{reference: SequenceRef, start, end, strand}` — interbase 0-based half-open internally (the VRS convention; kills the off-by-one class at one seam); surface 1-based input only under an explicit `coords:` annotation, converted deterministically and receipt-recorded; **four named fail-closed errors**, one per missing component (no reference / unversioned reference / no coordinate convention / no strand where the SO feature kind is stranded). Variant alleles are the one strand-free case by construction (VRS's own design).

**Identifier instability `[proposal]`:** the middle policy, made safe by pinning — the IR stores only version-fixed identifiers (accession.version, stable-ID.version, GCA/GCF.v, HGNC:#, taxid+taxdump release, CVCL+release); the **surface accepts human symbols only under an imported, pinned nomenclature snapshot**, resolving symbol → HGNC:# → selected product accession with the chain in the receipt, erroring (with candidates listed) on withdrawn/ambiguous/absent symbols. HGNC's own guidance is the authority: symbols change, HGNC IDs never do. Refusing symbols entirely would buy no soundness (resolution under a pinned snapshot is deterministic) at real usability cost. Taxids compile against a pinned taxdump: merged.dmp rewrites are applied and receipted (the authority's own equivalence), delnodes.dmp taxids are hard errors. **Cell lines are never organisms**: CellLine is a distinct type keyed by Cellosaurus CVCL (=RRID); problematic-list status (~8.6% of literature lines) enters the context record and every dependent verdict's basis — the A.5.6 visibility pattern applied to specimen identity.

**Entity lattice `[proposal]`:** five distinct nominal types with **no implicit coercion** — Sequence (a digested string, nothing more), Feature (SO-typed region; SO mandatory, as GFF3 already requires), Gene (locus; has products and features via typed edges, no sequence of its own), Transcript, Protein — "TP53 inhibits MDM2" fails closed until the author says which kind each name denotes, with the error listing the known products. VRS classes (Allele, CisPhasedBlock, CopyNumber, structural) transcribe as IR types.

**Defeasible biology — the second pre-registered exhibit `[proposal]`:** biological generalizations are **never bare universals**. Three declared quantifier forms: (a) `universal` — only for relations on a schema-maintained whitelist (definitional/mathematical); (b) `default_in(context, except: ExceptionClass)` — a Cyc-style context-scoped default compiling, inside an assembled context, to a **classical guarded implication** whose exception relation is open-world (verdicts capped at `supported`) unless owner-closed per A.5.6 (upgrading to proved-in-context, closure cited); lifting a default out of its context without a declared lift = `out_of_regime`; GO's NOT-qualifier practice is the transcription precedent — exceptions are curated positive assertions, never inferred; (c) `statistical(population, measure, value, uncertainty)` — bound to a first-class cohort entity. A bare ∀ over a biological kind is `E_BIO_UNSCOPED_UNIVERSAL`, an error naming all three repairs. This keeps the core monotonic and classical — the anti-Cyc invariant — while making biology's exception structure first-class. Whether guarded implications suffice for *real* curated rule sets (no exception-of-exception explosion) is the biology analogue of the uncertainty trigger: **pre-registered as a B.9/Stage E exhibit**.

**G2P, homology, pathways, artifacts `[proposal]`:** genotype–phenotype and homology are dedicated claim kinds with mandatory ECO evidence codes and pinned-vocabulary terms — they **never compile to implications**; function transfer across a homology edge requires an explicit declared lift. Pathways in v1 are named versioned *collections/contexts* importing reactions and interaction claims — never first-class causal objects (the Path2Models uncurated-volume trap). Large artifacts: the **semantic threshold** — in the KB iff it can appear as a term the reasoner may destructure; external otherwise, via one `ExternalArtifact` type (media type + declared schema + digest + size + retrieval-as-hint-never-identity), GA4GH digests where the class defines one, sha-256 otherwise; dangling or mismatched references fail at context assembly. The BioModels decay record (dangling files, NaN parameters inside the *curated* corpus; 88% recovered after repair) is the controlling precedent.

**Governance ruling (architect, for Gate 1 ratification):** curated OBO ontologies (SO, OBI, GO, HPO, MONDO) **qualify under transcription-only governance**, with the rule's intent clarified: the mechanizable oracle is *"term T exists in release R with definition D and status S"* — existence and identity, which the product transcribes — while term *correctness* is the registry's curatorial responsibility, not the product's. The rule bars in-house judgment curation; it does not bar binding to externally-governed judgment. `[proposal]`

*Confidence: high on coordinates/identity/versioning; medium on the generalization typing, which is consistent with every Stage A ruling but unproven at scale. Largest unverified assumption: that (context scope × exception relation × statistical claim) carries real curated biological rule sets without combinatorial guard explosion — if not, the biology IR either grows a stratified-default layer (breaking the no-default-logic invariant) or fails Gate 1 on defeasibility; pre-registered as an exhibit.*

## B.7 The uncertainty algebra and the yeast exhibition

Evidence: `stage-b-evidence/uncertainty.md` — every normative mechanic verified against text extracted from the official JCGM PDFs (GUM 100 + Amd.1, 101, 102, GUM-6, VIM3, NIST TN 1297) this session; the exhibition computed three independent ways (covariance-form LPU, shared-influence LPU, Monte Carlo at seed 20260826) agreeing to four decimals and matching Stage A's red-team arithmetic. `[local-fact: computations this session]`

### Trigger verdict: **the four-kind separation survives — the pre-registered don't-build trigger does not fire.** `[proposal, exhibited]`

Under first-order law-of-propagation (LPU), the output budget is **exactly additive per component and per kind** — measurement and parameter subtotals, numerical components discharged by certificate, structural as markers — including under declared correlation via shared-influence rows. The one honest degradation: a joint Monte Carlo run admits no exact per-component decomposition; per-kind shares are recovered by grouped variance-based sensitivity at declared extra cost, typed **estimate-class** with a disclosed interaction residual. That is a *typed, disclosed weakening, not a conflation* — and it is registered that this MCM budget mechanism is this report's construction, not JCGM-normative; if the interaction residual dominates for strongly nonlinear models, the question reopens at that vertical, not at the yeast slice (the evidence file's largest-unverified-assumption, carried forward verbatim).

## B.8 Contexts, claims, and lifting

Evidence: `stage-b-evidence/contexts.md` (McCarthy IJCAI-93 text-extracted; nanopub guidelines, VA-Spec, SEPIO, PROV-O, OBO, Wikidata, Bleiholder–Naumann — primary-sourced 2026-08-26). All recommendations adopted; three rulings made.

**Lifting `[proposal]`:** lifting *axioms* (arbitrary formulas — undecidable to check; the part McCarthy left unsettled and Cyc left to argumentation) are replaced by lifting **declarations**: `import R from C via T`, with T from a **closed typed transform catalog** — v1: identity, argument-extension with fixed binding (McCarthy's own canonical on(x,y)→on(x,y,s) example — lifting is signature-changing, so inheritance-only would fail Gate 1), parameter-specialization, and unit/frame recontextualization (delegated to the quantity system). Exactly **one implicit lift**: unchanged inheritance down a declared specialization DAG with assumption-monotone context records (Cyc's genlMt — the one mode that ran deterministically for decades). A lift compiles iff three statically-evaluable conditions hold (assumption compatibility over typed context records; transform totality or discharged domain-of-definition; target regime satisfaction) — lifting becomes typed function application, no search, no defaults. **The failed lift is a structured, blameable artifact** — claim + both context versions + every candidate path with its per-path failure (no_lift_declared | assumption_violated{var, required, actual} | transform_partial | regime_violated) — and *is* the `out_of_regime` payload; the missing-context-variable case routes to `invalid_input` instead, keeping author-fault and world-answer distinct. Queries follow McCarthy's enter/discharge: enter exactly one assembled context, classical inference inside, every verdict exported context-stamped — **there is no context-free verdict type and no root "universal" context** (a dumping ground whose assumptions are never checked). Lifting declarations are versioned, owned (by the *importing* context), cited in every dependent basis, and superseded like claims.

**Claims `[proposal]`:** the 17 kinds' mandatory fields are enforced as **schema-required fields in typed claim constructors** — the GA4GH VA-Spec mechanism (the *only* fetched precedent that machine-enforces per-kind required fields, at 3 fields on one class) generalized to the product's breadth, which the survey confirms is novel: nanopub's MUSTs are structural, micropub is minimal-by-design, OWL provably cannot fail closed on absence. The claim record layout adopts nanopublication's trisection (assertion / provenance / publication-info) as **native typed records, not RDF**; claim identity is content-addressed (the envelope digest convention); PROV-O qualified patterns serve as the export rendering.

**Supersession and conflict `[proposal]`:** claims immutable; two authority-bound edges — `supersedes(new→old, reason)` and `retracts(old, reason)` with reason codes seeded from IAO's obsolescence enum; **nothing recomputes automatically** (every shipped system resolves staleness at read time); re-reads against "latest" annotate `stale_basis` (citable, flagged) vs `basis_retracted` (barred from serving as basis in new derivations until re-evaluated). Three rulings on the evidence file's open questions: **closure never lifts** (importing a closed relation imports its extension, not its closure — a closure is the owner's claim about *their* context only); **a retraction cannot be retracted in v1** (re-assertion is a new claim); **`prefer_newer` reads a per-kind designated timestamp field** (measurement date for measurements, publication date for citations — fixed in each kind's schema, else the policy is ambiguous). The v1 conflict-policy set is exactly the Stage A three — `prefer_newer`, `prefer_source_rank(total_order)`, `manual(claim_id)` — all Bleiholder–Naumann "deciding" strategies; **"mediating" strategies (averaging) are banned** (they fabricate unsourced values) as is frequency voting (rewards copy-count); policies must be total over the conflict set or return `conflicting` — never guess; receipts record policy id + version, full conflict set, winner, and per-loser exclusion reason (the Wikidata reason-qualifier pattern, made automatic — the survey's one genuinely novel corner).

*Confidence: high on mechanisms (each traces to a fetched primary source plus a standing ruling). Largest unverified assumption: that the closed four-transform catalog covers the vertical's real cross-context lifts (in-vitro→in-vivo, regime narrowing, condition binding) — no deterministic closed-catalog lifting system exists in production anywhere; pre-registered as a primary B.9 battery target, with the honest fallback being a v2 user-defined-transform tier behind a certificate obligation.*
