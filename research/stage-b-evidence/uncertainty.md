## subject

Uncertainty algebra for the Stage B IR — GUM/JCGM-bound component typing, correlated propagation, budget artifacts, and the pre-registered don't-build-trigger exhibition on the yeast slice

## findings

STANDARDS MECHANICS, verified from extracted primary text (all accessed 2026-08-26). Correlated inputs: JCGM 100:2008 §5.2.2 gives the law of propagation with covariance — u_c²(y) = ΣΣ (∂f/∂x_i)(∂f/∂x_j) u(x_i,x_j), eq (13), with the pairwise correlation coefficient r(x_i,x_j) = u(x_i,x_j)/(u(x_i)u(x_j)), eq (14), and the correlation-coefficient form eq (16). Covariance evaluation is itself Type A (paired simultaneous observations, eq 17) or Type B (§5.2.5). Crucially, §5.2.4–5.2.5 sanction the ALTERNATIVE representation: introduce the common influence (same thermometer, same standard, ambient T, P) as an additional independent input quantity, and the correlation disappears from the declaration because it becomes structural. JCGM 102:2011 generalizes to matrices: U_y = C_x U_x C_xᵀ (eq 3) with U_x the input covariance matrix; its §3.20 Note 4 concedes a declared U_x may fail positive-definiteness and describes eigenvalue "repair" — for a fail-closed compiler that is a refusal condition, not a repair. Degrees of freedom: GUM G.4.1 gives Welch-Satterthwaite (G.2b) for effective dof, valid only when u_c² is a plain sum of independent components; G.3 supplies finite dof for Type B components via ν ≈ ½(Δu/u)⁻²; G.4 Note 3 shows the standard itself decomposing u_c² into Type-A and Type-B subtotals with separate ν_effA/ν_effB — the germ of per-kind budget subtotals. JCGM 102 §9.4.2.8 states flatly that W-S is NOT valid when finite-dof quantities are not independent — there is no standardized correlated-case effective dof; the standards route to MCM instead. JCGM 101:2008 is required when (a) linearization is inadequate or (b) the output PDF departs appreciably from Gaussian/scaled-t (Introduction; validity conditions §5.10.1: f continuous, output DF strictly increasing, unimodal PDF for shortest intervals, E(Y),V(Y) exist, M sufficient); it defines the adaptive procedure (§7.9.4: batches of M = max(J,10⁴), stabilization when 2×the Monte-Carlo standard deviation of y, u(y), y_low, y_high each falls below the numerical tolerance δ), the tolerance rule (§7.9.2: δ = ½·10^ℓ of the last meaningful digit of u(y)), two coverage-interval types (probabilistically symmetric vs shortest, §5.3.4), and §8's validation recipe: run GUM-framework and MCM, compare interval endpoints against δ. JCGM 100/Amd.1:2026 ("Nonlinearity in measurement models") is the live normative anchor for linearity adequacy. The BUDGET as artifact: VIM3 (JCGM 200:2012) 2.33 defines uncertainty budget = "statement of a measurement uncertainty, of the components..., and of their calculation and combination", to include the model, estimates, uncertainties, covariances, PDF types, dof, evaluation type, coverage factor — this note is effectively the row schema of the product's budget type. GUM §7.1.4(b) ("list all uncertainty components and document fully how they were evaluated") and NIST TN 1297 (1994) Table D.1 (rows = source, standard uncertainty, (A)/(B) mark) are the reporting precedents. GUM-6:2020 matters twice: §8.6 treats numerical inaccuracy as something to eliminate, not to budget; §11.10.4 recommends folding model-selection uncertainty into the combined uncertainty as "just another contribution" — the product deliberately REFUSES that (A.5.7: structural is never a number), a documented departure from a JCGM document.

TRIGGER VERDICT: the four-kind separation SURVIVES the slice. Under first-order LPU the output budget is exactly additive per component and per kind (measurement/parameter subtotals; numerical excluded by the dominance rule; structural as markers), including under declared correlation via shared-influence rows. The one honest degradation is joint Monte Carlo: M fused samples admit no exact per-component decomposition; per-kind shares must be re-estimated (grouped variance-based sensitivity), cost extra runs, and for nonlinear models leave an interaction residual — the budget drops from exact to estimate-class and must say so. That is a typed, disclosed weakening, not a conflation; no don't-build trigger fires. NUMERICAL vs MEASUREMENT: an enclosure half-width is a sup-norm sure bound with a certificate; a standard uncertainty is a dispersion parameter; the typed rule is dominance-vs-δ (JCGM 101 §7.9.2's own tolerance), never RSS — below δ the numerical component is discharged-with-certificate in the budget; above δ, any comparison verdict inside the contested band is indeterminate{source: numerical}. The same rule discharges interval-typed parameters (the CIAAW molar-mass case), unifying exactness classes 2–3 and epistemic intervals under one composition discipline.

## design_recommendations

- **decision**: Measured-quantity type = value + unit + typed component list (the budget IS the type) | **recommendation**: A measured quantity is {value, unit, components: [{id, kind: measurement|numerical|parameter|structural-marker, evaluation: TypeA{n, dof}|TypeB{basis-citation}, characterization: distribution(family, params)|interval(lo,hi)|enclosure-certificate, dof: finite(ν)|infinite (mandatory, no default), correlation: none|shared-influence-ref|declared-edge}]}. Mirror VIM3 2.33's note as the row schema: estimates, uncertainties, covariances, PDF types, dof, evaluation type, coverage factor. | **rationale**: VIM 2.33 + GUM 7.1.4(b) + TN 1297 Table D.1 already prescribe exactly this content for human reports; mechanizing the budget as the value type is transcription-only governance, and the yeast slice showed every field load-bearing (dof gated the W-S step; evaluation-type marked every table row; interval characterization carried the CIAAW molar mass). | **alternatives_rejected**: A single (value, sigma) pair — GUM 7.2.2 itself warns the bare ± form is ambiguous; Stan's everything-is-a-distribution — the Stage A survey showed it erases the measurement/parameter distinction irrecoverably.
- **decision**: Correlation is structural first; pairwise declarations are boundary-only | **recommendation**: Represent shared influences (T, P, a shared calibration standard) as single nodes in the quantity DAG; covariance between derived quantities is then computed, exact, and attributable as per-influence budget rows. Accept declared covariance/correlation edges (GUM eq 13/14, JCGM 102 U_x) only on ingestion-boundary inputs whose common ancestry is outside the system, with Type A (eq 17, paired observations) or Type B provenance mandatory on the edge. Declaring an edge between quantities that already share an in-graph ancestor is a compile error (double counting). A declared U_x that is not positive semidefinite is a refusal, never an eigenvalue 'repair'. | **rationale**: GUM 5.2.4–5.2.5 explicitly bless the shared-influence formulation; the exhibition proved numerical identity of the two routes (u(S) = 1.4784 both ways) while the structural route yields attributable budget rows (P +0.11, T −0.04) instead of an unattributable −2cov lump. JCGM 102's own PSD-repair note (3.20 Note 4) marks the failure mode fail-closed compilation must refuse. | **alternatives_rejected**: Correlation-matrix-first interfaces (JCGM 102 style as primary): loses per-influence attribution, invites inconsistent/non-PSD declarations, and makes the double-declaration error unstatable.
- **decision**: Propagation operators are declared per computation, typed, and versioned | **recommendation**: No default propagation. Operators: lpu{order: 1, covariance: from-graph} (GUM eqs 10/13/16); mcm{trials: fixed(M)|adaptive{ndig}, seed, rng-id} (JCGM 101 §7.2/§7.9.4); enclosure{method-id} (certificate-bearing interval arithmetic); nested{outer: interval-scan|optimization, inner: mcm|lpu} for mixed aleatory/epistemic (Dakota second-order probability). Output types differ by operator: lpu → (estimate, u_c, exact budget); mcm → (estimate, u, coverage-interval tagged probabilistically-symmetric|shortest, replayable seed, estimate-class budget); enclosure → certified interval (verdict-side: proved-with-certificate per A.4); nested → p-box. | **rationale**: JCGM 101's Introduction gives the normative conditions splitting lpu from mcm; the coverage-interval type split (5.3.4) and the seed/trials parameters are what make an MCM result replayable — the receipts requirement. Dakota's nested semantics is debugged, published prior art (Stage A A.8.15). | **alternatives_rejected**: Silent always-LPU (spreadsheet practice — fails JCGM 101 case (a)); always-MCM (loses closed-form auditability, injects stochasticity where the model is linear; the slice validated lpu to 0.1%).
- **decision**: LPU applicability is a checked regime with a cacheable MCM validation certificate | **recommendation**: lpu carries validity predicates (differentiability at the estimate; linearity adequacy; Gaussianity adequacy for any coverage statement). Discharge them either by a declared adequacy justification recorded in the budget, or by an attached JCGM 101 §8 validation run (GUM-framework vs adaptive MCM, endpoints compared against the §7.9.2 tolerance δ) cached as a certificate keyed on model + input hashes. An undischarged predicate is a compile error; a failed comparison retypes the computation to mcm. | **rationale**: JCGM 101 §8.1.1 recommends exactly this dual-run comparison 'in any cases of doubt'; Amd.1:2026 (nonlinearity) is the live normative hook. The exhibition performed the validation (u agreement within δ) — it is cheap for slice-scale models. | **alternatives_rejected**: Trusting an author's linearity assertion with no artifact (the V&V-culture failure Stage A documented: enforcement by convention does not hold); requiring MCM validation unconditionally (wasteful; §8.1.1 says favourable comparison extends to 'sufficiently similar problems').
- **decision**: dof mandatory; Welch-Satterthwaite gated on independence of finite-dof components | **recommendation**: Every component declares dof ∈ (0, ∞]; Type A carries its statistical dof; Type B declares ∞ explicitly or derives finite ν via GUM G.3 (ν ≈ ½(Δu/u)⁻²) with the reliability judgment recorded. ν_eff via G.2b is computed only when all finite-dof components are mutually independent; otherwise the compiler refuses t-based expanded uncertainty citing JCGM 102 §9.4.2.8 and demands mcm or an explicit coverage policy. Expanded uncertainty is always the triple (k, source: t{ν_eff}|gaussian|declared-policy, p) — never a bare k = 2. | **rationale**: G.4's formula presupposes u_c² = Σu_i² (independent); JCGM 102 §9.4.2.8 states the correlated case is invalid and offers no substitute — the standards' own gap must surface as a typed refusal, not a silent k = 2. The exhibition's ν_eff = 15.2 → t = 2.13 shows a 7% coverage error hiding in the k = 2 habit. | **alternatives_rejected**: Default k = 2 (ubiquitous lab practice; a policy, so it must be declared as one); inventing a correlated-case effective-dof formula (no oracle — violates transcription-only governance).
- **decision**: Budgets are exact and per-kind additive under LPU; every output carries one | **recommendation**: Every propagated quantity emits a machine-readable budget: rows (component-id, kind, evaluation, characterization, signed contribution u_i(y) = c_i·u(x_i), variance share, dof), per-kind subtotals (u²_meas, u²_param), shared-influence rows for correlations, and discharged rows ('certified below tolerance δ') for dominance-dismissed components. A collapsed sigma with no budget is not a constructible result value. | **rationale**: GUM G.4 Note 3 already decomposes u_c² into Type-A/Type-B subtotals — per-kind subtotals are the same move; additivity of variance shares under first-order LPU makes the budget exact (all exhibition tables sum to 100.0%). Keeping discharged rows visible is what makes negligibility auditable instead of assumed (the molar-mass row). | **alternatives_rejected**: Budget-on-demand recomputation (loses the receipt property — the budget must be the artifact that shipped with the verdict); collapsing sub-dominant rows (silent, and the ratchet by which components disappear).
- **decision**: Numerical error composes with measurement uncertainty by dominance-vs-δ, never RSS | **recommendation**: Numerical-kind components (exactness class 2: enclosure with certificate; class 3: estimate) carry sup-norm interval semantics. Composition rule: compare the enclosure half-width (class 3: the estimate, flagged) against JCGM 101 §7.9.2's tolerance δ = ½·10^ℓ of the last meaningful digit of u_c(y). Below δ: discharged-with-certificate in the budget, verdict proceeds on u_c. Above δ: any comparison verdict decided inside the contested band returns indeterminate{source: numerical, margin}; the fix is more precision, not a wider sigma. The same rule discharges interval-typed parameters (CIAAW molar-mass intervals). | **rationale**: δ is the GUM ecosystem's own boundary for 'numerically meaningful' (JCGM 101 uses it to stop MCM adaptation and to validate the GUM framework in §8.1.3); GUM-6 §8.6 treats numerical inaccuracy as to-be-eliminated, not budgeted. An enclosure width and a standard deviation answer different questions (guaranteed containment vs dispersion) — no sound RSS exists. | **alternatives_rejected**: width/√12 rectangularization then RSS into u_c — launders a sure bound into a probability statement and is precisely the kind-conflation the workstream was pre-registered to catch; ignoring numerical error when 'small' without the certificate row (unauditable).
- **decision**: MCM budgets are estimate-class with a disclosed interaction residual | **recommendation**: After a joint mcm run, per-kind and per-component shares are recovered by grouped variance-based sensitivity (first-order and total effects per kind-group or influence-group), costing declared extra model runs; for nonlinear models the shares need not sum to 100% and the residual is an explicit 'interaction' budget line. The whole decomposition is typed estimate (class 3) and marked so, never presented as exact. | **rationale**: This is the one place the four-kind separation honestly degrades: M fused samples admit no exact decomposition. Typing the degradation (exact → estimate, with residual) preserves the separation as a disclosed property instead of silently pretending LPU-grade budgets. This finding is the workstream's answer to the trigger question: a typed weakening, not a conflation. | **alternatives_rejected**: Refusing budgets under MCM (throws away the auditability exactly where JCGM 101 is mandatory); one-at-a-time re-runs presented as shares (wrong under correlation/nonlinearity, no residual disclosure).
- **decision**: Aleatory/epistemic: intervals are a distinct characterization, never auto-coerced | **recommendation**: A component becomes an interval when the author declares epistemic-interval (bounds-only warrant, Dakota discipline); GUM Type B with bounds-only knowledge may instead declare a rectangular distribution (GUM 4.3.7) — a belief encoding. The two are different types: interval components are ineligible for lpu and mcm (compile error naming the component and the two legal moves: declare a distribution with recorded justification, or switch to enclosure/nested). Mixed sets type as nested propagation yielding p-boxes (CDF-bound pairs) whose interval dimension is attributable to the epistemic components and whose distributional dimension to the aleatory ones. | **rationale**: Dakota's aleatory-vs-epistemic variable typing with nested second-order probability is shipped, debugged semantics (Stage A A.8.15); GUM/JCGM-101 by contrast put ALL Type B knowledge into PDFs — the product must hold both, and the choice is semantic (unknown-in-[a,b] vs uniformly-distributed-on-[a,b]), so it must be authorial and explicit. | **alternatives_rejected**: Auto-rectangularization of intervals (silently converts an epistemic non-claim into a probabilistic claim); Dempster-Shafer BPA structures in v1 (adds a third calculus with no committed consumer; revisit if a competency question demands belief/plausibility).
- **decision**: Structural uncertainty: marker components only; refuse GUM-6 11.10.4 model averaging | **recommendation**: structural-marker components carry model-id + version and no magnitude; they condition the result ('conditional on: ideal-gas v…, complete-respiration stoichiometry v…') and flow into every downstream budget untouched. Alternative models yield separate result rows with per-model verdicts; no averaging, no numeric structural contribution. Record in GUARANTEES-equivalent documentation that this deliberately departs from JCGM GUM-6:2020 §11.10.4, which folds model uncertainty into the combined uncertainty as 'just another contribution'. | **rationale**: Stage A ruling A.5.7 (structural never a number in v1) + the stacking-vs-BMA literature (M-open incoherence, Yao et al. 2018) + the trigger discipline: a numeric structural term is exactly the collapse the seven-kind separation forbids. The departure must be documented because GUM-6 is otherwise an adopted source — an undocumented divergence from an adopted standard is an audit finding waiting to happen. | **alternatives_rejected**: GUM-6 model averaging into the budget; a scalar 'model uncertainty' u_struct (no oracle, no agreed semantics).
- **decision**: Constants and molar masses are parameter-kind with mandatory provenance; exactness is a first-class value | **recommendation**: CODATA-exact constants (R, k, N_A, h since SI 2019) type as parameter with u = 0 and citation — the zero is a stated fact, not an omission. IUPAC/CIAAW interval atomic weights type as epistemic intervals; within-molecule repetition (6 C atoms) is perfectly correlated by construction — the shared-influence representation handles it for free (one node per element). Even discharged components remain budget rows. | **rationale**: The exhibition showed the machinery end-to-end: R contributed a true zero row; M(glucose) ∈ [180.1459, 180.1655] contributed ≤0.00055 mmol, discharged against δ = 0.005 — negligibility was computed and certified, not assumed. Transcription-only governance holds: every entry has a mechanizable oracle (CODATA table, CIAAW table). | **alternatives_rejected**: Baking constants in as bare literals (loses versioned-evidence provenance, breaks when CODATA revises); treating atomic-weight intervals as ± Gaussians (misstates CIAAW's own semantics of natural-variation intervals).

## adopt

JCGM 100:2008 (GUM) with Amd.1:2026 as the LPU/correlation/dof normative base (eqs 10, 13–16, 17; G.2b, G.3, G.4 Note 3); JCGM 101:2008 for Monte Carlo propagation (validity conditions 5.10.1, adaptive procedure 7.9.4, numerical tolerance 7.9.2, coverage-interval types 5.3.4, GUM-framework validation §8); JCGM 102:2011 for the multivariate form U_y = C_x·U_x·C_xᵀ and the W-S-invalid-under-correlation ruling (9.4.2.8); JCGM GUM-6:2020 for measurement-model development and the numerical-inaccuracy stance (§8.6) — while explicitly refusing its §11.10.4 model-averaging recommendation; VIM3 (JCGM 200:2012) definition 2.33 as the budget row schema; NIST TN 1297 (1994) Table D.1 as the budget-table rendering precedent; CODATA 2022 exact constants (R = 8.314 462 618… J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹, exact); CIAAW 2021 standard-atomic-weight intervals (H [1.00784, 1.00811], C [12.0096, 12.0116], O [15.99903, 15.99977]); Dakota 6.24's aleatory-distribution vs epistemic-interval variable typing with nested second-order probability for mixed propagation (per stage-a-evidence/dakota-vv-uq.md, primary docs re-verified there 2026-08-26).

## avoid

Collapsing the budget to one sigma (GUM 7.2.2 itself flags the bare ± as ambiguous; failure: unauditable results, the Stage A trigger's definition of collapse). RSS-ing enclosure widths or interval half-widths into u_c via width/√12 (failure: a sure bound laundered into a dispersion claim — kind conflation). Applying Welch-Satterthwaite over correlated finite-dof components (JCGM 102 9.4.2.8 says invalid; failure: fictitious coverage probabilities). Defaulting the coverage factor to k = 2 without a declared policy (failure: 7% coverage error at ν_eff ≈ 15 in the exhibition alone). 'Repairing' a non-PSD declared covariance matrix instead of refusing it (JCGM 102 3.20 Note 4's own workaround; failure: silently altered declarations). Declaring pairwise correlations for quantities whose common ancestor is already in the graph (failure: double-counted covariance, exactly what the shared-influence check catches). GUM-6 11.10.4-style model averaging into the combined uncertainty (failure: destroys the structural-kind separation; incoherent in M-open settings). Auto-coercing epistemic intervals to rectangular PDFs (failure: converts a non-claim into a probabilistic claim the author never made). Presenting MCM sensitivity-derived budget shares as exact (failure: nonlinear interaction residual silently attributed to the wrong kind).

## open_questions

- Budget attribution for boundary-declared covariance edges (no shared node available): pair-rows vs 50/50 split vs a dedicated 'joint' row — needs a convention before the receipt schema freezes (Stage D).
- Cost policy for MCM estimate-class budgets: which grouped variance-based sensitivity estimator, how many extra runs, and whether the residual line has a refusal threshold (an interaction share so large the per-kind budget is meaningless).
- Verdict semantics over p-box outputs: when is refuted/supported decidable on CDF bounds, and what margin payload does indeterminate carry when the band, not a sigma, is the obstruction?
- Mixing certificate-bound (enclosure) and t-interval (dof-bearing) quantities in ONE comparison verdict: the dominance rule handles small numerical components, but a comparison where both sides are large and differently-typed has no worked exhibition yet.
- ISO/IEC 17025 reporting-language conformance (clause-level uncertainty-reporting requirements) unverified — full text paywalled; decide whether budget rendering should claim 17025-compatible wording (evidence-incomplete).
- Whether Stage E's Gate-0-approved restatement of O2-consumed as a volume at shared T,P (per stage-a.md W15 fix) moves the covariance term into the limiting-reagent margin itself — it would make property 2 bite in the slice's headline number instead of the auxiliary difference; recompute at Stage E.
- Amd.1:2026's concrete normative changes to GUM linearity guidance were not extracted (amendment PDF not fetched) — verify before wiring the lpu adequacy predicates to specific clauses.
