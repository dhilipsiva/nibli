## subject

Physics domain IR requirements (Stage B §5): unit/dimension semantics, constants, frames, tensors/spinors, physics-object validation classes, and regime machinery

## findings

UNITS — WHAT THE FOUR STANDARDS ACTUALLY SAY. SI Brochure 9th ed. (BIPM; PDF stamped V4.01, June 2026, CC-BY 4.0) defines dimension as a 7-exponent product dim Q = T^α L^β M^γ I^δ Θ^ε N^ζ J^η with "generally small integer" exponents (§2.3.3). Radian/steradian are quantities with the unit one "for reasons of history and convention" — and Table 4 footnotes (b),(c) concede rad = m/m "is not intrinsic and may be misleading since angle is not the same kind of quantity as other length ratios." The Metrologia literature splits three ways: SI status quo; Quincey (angle as base quantity, rad base unit, sr = rad²); Mohr/Phillips/Shirley/Trott (dimensional angle requiring SI changes). §5.4.7: ratio quantities may optionally carry m/m, mol/mol to disambiguate; number-of-entities quantities may NOT, and "descriptive terms … in place of the unit one" are explicitly outside the SI, with documentation the only enforcement — precisely the fidelity-test failure class. Celsius is affine: t = T − 273.15 K; temperature differences are numerically equal in K and °C (Table 4 note f). Neper/bel/decibel are accepted-for-use non-SI with the normative demand that "the quantity be specified, and that any reference value used be specified" (Table 8 note m); dB carries the decadic log of power ratios, Np the natural log of amplitude ratios — the 10-vs-20 factor is a property of the QUANTITY (power vs root-power), not the unit. ISO 80000-1:2022 (paywalled; ISO/ANSI/BSI summaries) replaced the 2009 edition, refocused on ISQ concepts, removed reproduced SI/constants content, and normatively covers logarithmic quantities and rounding — the residue behind the paywall is evidence-incomplete. UCUM v2.2 (2024-06-17) is the only one of the four with an executable algebra: "special units" are triples (u, f_s, f_s⁻¹) for interval/logarithmic scales (Cel = cel(1 K), f = x − 273.15) and "cannot take part in any algebraic operations, neither involving other units, nor themselves (exponentiation) nor involving scalars" (§22.1); arbitrary units are semantically opaque and mutually incomparable (§24–25); annotations {…} are "meaningless by definition" and discarded by conformant parsers; radian is a UCUM BASE unit (contradicting SI) and the mole is demoted to a dimensionless Avogadro count (contradicting SI and awkward for the chemistry vertical). QUDT (nonprofit; ontology, updated 2022) models DEG_C with conversionMultiplier 1.0 + conversionOffset 273.15 and qudt:scalingOf kelvin; DeciB is typed qudt:LogarithmicUnit with a D1 slot in its dimension vector and conversionMultiplier 0.0 — an explicit confession that its linear-conversion machinery cannot compute logarithmic units; reference quantities are smeared across multiple quantity kinds (SoundPressureLevel, …). QUDT's value is the IRI namespace and quantity-kind graph, not executable semantics. CONSEQUENCE: no single standard can be adopted verbatim; the product needs its OWN normative small-step semantics (a typed scale-class system over a dimension vector plus a quantity-kind layer), with each standard assigned a role: SI = ratio-scale kernel + rendering; ISO 80000 = quantity-kind names/definitions; UCUM = fail-closed I/O grammar; QUDT = identifiers. This satisfies transcription-only governance: every unit entry cites an external oracle, but the ALGEBRA is the product's spec, itself citing UCUM §21–25 and SI §2.3.3/5.4.7 clause by clause.

CONSTANTS. The current adjustment is CODATA 2022 (Mohr, Newell, Taylor, Tiesinga; Rev. Mod. Phys. 97, 025002, published 2025-04-30; NIST cuu content last updated May 2024). The seven SI defining constants are exact by definition since 2019: Δν_Cs = 9 192 631 770 Hz, c = 299 792 458 m/s, h = 6.626 070 15×10⁻³⁴ J·s, e = 1.602 176 634×10⁻¹⁹ C, k = 1.380 649×10⁻²³ J/K, N_A = 6.022 140 76×10²³ mol⁻¹, K_cd = 683 lm/W; NIST's "adopted values" category adds conventional exacts (g_n, standard atmosphere, standard-state pressure) and lists ħ — exact only as the symbolic expression h/2π, an irrational number. Measured constants carry GUM uncertainties: G = 6.674 30(15)×10⁻¹¹ m³ kg⁻¹ s⁻² (u_r 2.2×10⁻⁵), α = 7.297 352 5643(11)×10⁻³ (u_r 1.6×10⁻¹⁰), all particle masses. The next adjustment is CODATA 2026 (literature deadline 2026-12-31) — so release-pinning and cache invalidation will be exercised within the product's first year. Exactness is release-relative metadata (h was measured pre-2019), so "exact" must be a field of the sourced claim, never a compiler builtin.

FRAMES. The honest mathematical object is manifold + atlas + transition maps, with a coordinate system as a fixed chart and differential forms as the chart-free calculus. Cost evidence: SageManifolds implements the full machinery (charts, open covers, automatic change-of-frame, non-parallelizable manifolds, p-forms, connections) and is mature (integrated in SageMath, v10.8) — but it is a CAS storing symbolic components per frame, not a validator; mathlib's manifolds (Gouëzel 2019; charted spaces + structure groupoids, manifolds with corners over arbitrary normed fields) show that fully rigorous chart machinery is a multi-year formalization even for specialists. For a compiler whose v1–v2 physics is fixed-background (reaction networks, thermodynamics, classical/quantum mechanics on flat space), full atlases buy nothing statically checkable that typed frame tags do not: a frame tag + declared transformation is checked (not documentation), and mixing frames without a declared transform is a compile error — passing the fidelity test. What frame tags cannot express (tensor fields on topologically nontrivial or curved manifolds without global frames) must return `unsupported`, never be mis-encoded.

TENSORS AND SPINORS. Abstract-index (Penrose) representation types each index slot by (vector space, variance, symmetry group of slots); contraction is then a TOTAL TYPED operation — defined exactly when two slots share a space with opposite variance — which is the fidelity-test criterion. Twenty years of prior art (xAct's abstract calculus + xPerm canonicalization; Cadabra's Young-projector multi-term symmetries) show the hard part is canonicalization for symbolic transformation, which validation does not need. Component-with-basis is what numeric backends consume; it is sound only as a LOWERING of abstract-index terms with a mandatory frame tag. Coordinate-free operator style is not needed in v1 and complicates equation statement. Spinors: the honest statement is that a spinor field is a section of a bundle over a spin structure; short of that, "typed representation indices of a declared group" (SL(2,C) dotted/undotted, Spin(3), Dirac) — the xAct/Spinors and Cadabra approach — is NOT a lie provided the background is flat or a global tetrad/vierbein is declared (the numerical-relativity discipline). Nontrivial spin structures are `unsupported`.

REGIMES. Stage A A.5.4 already fixes the mechanism (typed predicates over context variables; three-valued: inside / out_of_regime / undeterminable→fail-closed invalid_input). The physics addition: the ideal-gas Z-deviation regime must NOT be stated as |pV/nRT − 1| ≤ 0.02 over the model's own output (circular — the model certifies itself); it must be over independent context variables (reduced temperature/pressure T_r, p_r against cited corresponding-states bounds) or reference an imported compressibility/virial correlation carrying its own source and uncertainty. Regime predicates are therefore claims (sourced, versioned, owner-scoped) and regime checking can consume evidence — meaning a regime check can itself yield a margin, and composition of models intersects regimes.

## design_recommendations

- **decision**: Unit kernel: 8-dimension vector + scale-class + quantity-kind, product-normative semantics | **recommendation**: Define quantities as value×unit where a unit carries (i) a dimension vector over SI's seven base dimensions PLUS Angle as an eighth, (ii) a scale class (see next row), and (iii) a quantity-kind identifier; write the product's own normative algebra spec citing SI Brochure 9 §2.3.3/§5.4.7 and UCUM §21–25 clause-by-clause. | **rationale**: Dimension vectors alone cannot separate torque from energy, Hz from rad/s, or mole fraction from mass fraction; no external standard supplies a complete executable algebra (QUDT's dB conversionMultiplier is literally 0.0; SI has no algebra for special units; UCUM forbids all algebra on them). Transcription-only governance is satisfied because every ENTRY cites an oracle while the algebra is spec, not vocabulary. | **alternatives_rejected**: Adopt SI verbatim (no affine/log algebra, dimensionless angle loses checks its own footnote calls misleading); adopt UCUM verbatim (dimensionless mole collides with the chemistry vertical's amount-of-substance; annotations are semantically discarded — a documented-convention failure); adopt QUDT as semantics (interchange ontology, cannot compute affine/log units).
- **decision**: Five scale classes as types with fixed operation tables | **recommendation**: Ratio (full algebra), Affine (interval scales), Level (logarithmic), Count (entity-typed), Arbitrary (opaque imported evidence, mutually incomparable, never convertible). Multiplication/division/exponentiation are defined ONLY on Ratio; every other class has an explicit finite operation table. | **rationale**: UCUM §22.1 is two decades of deployed precedent that special units admit no algebra; making the restriction a type (not a runtime warning) is what fail-closed means here. Arbitrary units (assay-defined) will arrive via the bio vertical's lab-data imports and must stay incomparable per UCUM §24–25. | **alternatives_rejected**: One numeric unit type with runtime conversion errors (Python pint style — fails the fidelity test); banning non-ratio units outright (loses °C, dB, pH, all of which the verticals need).
- **decision**: Affine units: point/difference type split | **recommendation**: Every affine scale yields two types: AbsolutePoint (e.g. CelsiusTemperature) and Difference (TemperatureDifference). Legal ops: point−point→difference, point±difference→point, differences form a vector space; point+point, point×scalar, point in products are compile errors. Conversions are total affine maps (SI: t/°C = T/K − 273.15; QUDT's multiplier+offset pattern as interchange). | **rationale**: SI Brochure Table 4 note (f) states temperature differences are numerically equal in °C and K while absolute values differ by 273.15 — a fact only a type split captures; UCUM's special-unit mechanism converts values but cannot distinguish a 5 °C difference from a 5 °C temperature, a classic silent-error source in thermodynamic property code. | **alternatives_rejected**: UCUM-style single special unit (ambiguity above); kelvin-only internal normalization with °C as display sugar (loses the difference/point distinction at the surface, so the ambiguity re-enters at authoring time).
- **decision**: Logarithmic Level type with mandatory reference, base, and power/root-power convention | **recommendation**: Level = log_b(Q/Q_ref) as a type parameterized by (log base, power|root-power convention, reference quantity WITH units and value, underlying quantity kind). A bare 'dB' with no declared reference is a compile error; dBm, dB(SPL re 20 µPa), pH (−log10 of hydrogen-ion activity per IUPAC) are distinct instantiated types. Level arithmetic: level−level (same instantiation) is meaningful; level+level is only via an explicit named operation (e.g. cascade_gain, power_sum) that lowers to the linear domain. | **rationale**: SI Brochure Table 8 note (m) makes reference and quantity specification normative prose; the product makes it a type parameter. The 10-vs-20 factor is a property of power vs root-power quantities, so it must live in the type or dB conversions are unsound. QUDT demonstrates the failure mode: LogarithmicUnit with conversionMultiplier 0.0 and references smeared over quantity kinds. | **alternatives_rejected**: dB as a dimensionless ratio unit (breaks additivity semantics and the reference requirement); banning log units (pH is non-negotiable for the chemistry/biology vertical); treating each dB-flavor as an unrelated opaque unit (loses the total typed lowering to the linear domain).
- **decision**: Angle is an internal base dimension; SI-compliance by explicit erasure lift | **recommendation**: Carry Angle as an eighth base dimension (rad coherent unit, sr = rad²); provide a declared, cited context lift 'SI-erasure' (rad↦1) applied only at explicitly marked boundaries (rendering, export, import of SI-conformant sources); trig/exponential functions are typed to take Angle (or a phase type), not bare numbers. | **rationale**: SI's own Table 4 footnote (b) says rad = m/m 'is not intrinsic and may be misleading'; UCUM already ships radian as a base unit, so there is deployed precedent; a fail-closed compiler wants torque≠energy and Hz≠rad/s to be type errors, which dimensionless angle makes impossible. The controversy (Quincey vs Mohr/Phillips vs SI status quo, all in Metrologia) is real, so the design records BOTH renderings and makes the erasure a cited lift, matching the Stage A lifting discipline — an angle-mismatch after a refused lift is out_of_regime-style, not silently equated. | **alternatives_rejected**: SI-strict dimensionless radian (loses the checks; contradicts the product's fail-closed identity); making angle checking a lint (a convention enforced by documentation — a fidelity failure by the Gate 1 definition).
- **decision**: Counting quantities are entity-typed Counts, never bare unit one | **recommendation**: Count<E> where E is an entity type drawn from governed vocabulary (ChEBI/GO species, cells, photons, decays); Counts of different E are incomparable without a declared mapping; amount-of-substance (mol) remains its own SI dimension with N_A (exact) as the sole declared bridge Count<E>↔amount. | **rationale**: SI Brochure §5.4.7: number-of-entity quantities cannot even take the m/m disambiguation option, and entity-descriptive terms are non-SI with documentation as the only enforcement; UCUM annotations {RBC} are 'meaningless by definition' and discarded. Both standards therefore push exactly the information the fidelity test says must be typed into untyped prose — the product types it. | **alternatives_rejected**: UCUM's dimensionless mole (breaks the chemistry vertical's stoichiometry typing); bare dimensionless counts with annotations (the documented-convention failure mode verbatim).
- **decision**: Standard roles fixed: SI=semantics anchor, ISO 80000=quantity-kind definitions, UCUM=I/O grammar, QUDT=identifier namespace | **recommendation**: Cite SI Brochure 9 (V4.01, 2026) for the ratio kernel, coherence, prefixes, rendering; ISO 80000-1:2022 (+parts, via accessible summaries until purchased) for quantity-kind names and logarithmic-quantity definitions; accept/emit UCUM 2.2 strings at I/O boundaries with fail-closed handling (annotation present → error naming the annotation, unless a declared mapping to a Count/kind exists; arbitrary units → Arbitrary class); use QUDT IRIs as the public identifiers of units and quantity kinds in the evidence graph, never QUDT numeric conversion data as normative. | **rationale**: Each standard is authoritative for exactly one layer and demonstrably wrong or silent for others (QUDT can't compute dB; UCUM contradicts SI on mole/radian; SI has no machine grammar). Assigning roles keeps every vocabulary entry oracle-backed while preventing any standard's weak layer from leaking into semantics. | **alternatives_rejected**: Single-standard adoption (each fails at least one layer, documented in findings); inventing product-local unit identifiers (violates transcription-only governance).
- **decision**: Physical constants are a pinned, versioned evidence pack — never compiler builtins | **recommendation**: Ship constants as imported-database-assertion claims (A.5.1 kind): (symbol, value, exactness-or-GUM-uncertainty, correlation references, release=CODATA-2022 [RMP 97, 025002, 2025-04-30], URL, retrieval date). The seven defining constants are exact decimals; derived exacts (ħ=h/2π, R=N_A·k, F=N_A·e) are stored as symbolic expressions over exact constants, evaluated to requested precision; measured constants (G: 6.67430(15)e-11, u_r 2.2e-5; α: u_r 1.6e-10; masses) carry uncertainties and, where CODATA publishes them, correlation coefficients. Contexts pin the release; any release change invalidates caches (A.5.3 machinery). | **rationale**: Exactness is release-relative (h was measured before 2019-05-20), so it must be claim metadata; ħ is irrational, so 'exact' must be allowed to mean 'exact expression', or the store lies at some precision. CODATA 2026 lands within the product's first year (deadline 2026-12-31), so the invalidation path is exercised early and must be designed, not retrofitted. | **alternatives_rejected**: Hard-coded f64 constants (invisible version, silent staleness, precision lie for ħ/R); treating all constants as uncertain (defeats exact symbolic simplification the 2019 SI makes legitimate).
- **decision**: Frames v1–v2: typed frame tags + declared transformations; manifold/chart machinery reserved, not implemented | **recommendation**: Every vector/tensor/field quantity carries a frame tag; frames are declared objects (origin, orientation, motion class, metric signature where relevant) with declared, possibly time-dependent transformations; cross-frame mixing without a declared transform is a compile error. The IR spec DEFINES Frame as a fixed chart with a distinguished frame field, so the v3+ atlas generalization is additive. Curved backgrounds / no-global-frame cases return unsupported. | **rationale**: SageManifolds shows full chart machinery is buildable but is a symbolic CAS discipline (components per frame, automatic change-of-frame) rather than a validator; mathlib shows rigorous formalization (structure groupoids, 2019, expert-years) is far beyond v1 budget. Frame tags are checked types, so they pass the amended fidelity test; the honesty condition is that inexpressible cases fail as unsupported instead of being flattened. | **alternatives_rejected**: Full atlases in v1 (cost without v1 payoff — the vertical slice is reaction networks); no frame typing at all (frame errors become documentation conventions — a Gate 1 failure); coordinate-free-only representation (numeric backends require components, which must then carry frames anyway).
- **decision**: Tensors: abstract-index typed slots in the IR; frame-tagged components only as lowering | **recommendation**: Tensor types declare index slots typed by (vector space, co/contra variance, declared slot symmetries); contraction is a total typed operation defined exactly on same-space opposite-variance slot pairs; raising/lowering requires a declared metric. Numeric delegation lowers to component arrays tagged with a declared frame. Multi-term symmetry canonicalization (Young projectors) is deferred to the v2 symbolic-transformation layer, with xAct/xPerm and Cadabra as the named prior art. | **rationale**: Abstract-index makes index contraction the total typed operation Gate 1 demands, without committing to any basis; 20 years of xAct/Cadabra practice show validation-level typing is cheap and canonicalization is the only expensive part — which validation does not need. Molecular-graph/tensor non-collapse rule is honored: tensors are never generic predicates. | **alternatives_rejected**: Component-first representation (basis-dependence leaks into model identity; equality becomes frame-relative); fully coordinate-free operator calculus (awkward for stating component-level boundary conditions and for solver handoff); string-based index conventions (documentation-enforced — fidelity failure).
- **decision**: Spinors: typed representation indices over a declared Spin group, flat background or declared global tetrad; everything else unsupported | **recommendation**: Represent spinors as objects with index slots typed by declared group representations (SL(2,C) dotted/undotted Weyl, Dirac, SU(2)/Spin(3)), with typed epsilon/gamma intertwiners; on curved backgrounds admit spinors only when a global tetrad/vierbein is declared. Nontrivial spin structures (topologically nontrivial manifolds) are unsupported with that named capability. | **rationale**: This is the xAct/Spinors and Cadabra representation and the operational discipline of numerical relativity; it is NOT a lie within its declared scope because the spin bundle is trivial over a flat background or a global frame — the honesty is in refusing the cases where it isn't, rather than pretending bundle machinery exists. | **alternatives_rejected**: Full spin-bundle machinery (no prior art at validator level; cost unjustified before any QFT-on-curved-space use case); encoding spinors as complex column vectors with conventions in comments (documentation-enforced transformation law — fidelity failure); omitting spinors (Pauli/Dirac systems are inside the four-domain-wide physics IR mandate).
- **decision**: Regime predicates are sourced claims over independent context variables; self-referential regimes are refused | **recommendation**: A model law's validity regime is a conjunction of typed, unit-checked predicates over declared context variables, each predicate carrying a citation; the compiler REFUSES a regime predicate whose evaluation requires the model's own output (the ideal-gas |Z−1|≤0.02 stated via pV/nRT is the canonical refusal). The honest encodings offered: (a) bounds on reduced state variables (T_r, p_r) citing a corresponding-states source, or (b) a predicate referencing an imported compressibility/virial correlation as evidence with its own uncertainty — in which case the regime check itself returns a margin and its basis cites the correlation. Regimes intersect under model composition; the three-valued check (inside / out_of_regime / undeterminable→fail-closed) is the A.5.4 machinery unchanged. | **rationale**: The vertical slice depends on this example; the circularity refusal is the only new physics-side rule, and without it a model certifies its own applicability — a false-green generator. Making regime predicates claims keeps them versioned, owned, and citable in verdicts, consistent with closure discipline (A.5.6). | **alternatives_rejected**: Prose regime notes (unchecked — the Stage A decision already bans this); regime as a runtime warning (breaks out_of_regime as a verdict); allowing model-output-referencing regimes with a fixpoint interpretation (unsound self-certification, and unimplementable without solving the model to decide whether to trust the model).

## adopt

SI Brochure 9th edition, V4.01 (June 2026), CC-BY 4.0 — normative ratio-scale kernel, coherence, prefixes (incl. ronna/quetta, 27th CGPM 2022), rendering rules. ISO 80000-1:2022 — quantity-kind names and logarithmic-quantity definitions (via summaries until purchased). UCUM v2.2 (2024-06-17) — I/O unit-string grammar and the special/arbitrary-unit no-algebra precedent (§21–§25). QUDT (2022 ontology) — public IRIs for units/quantity kinds in the evidence graph only. CODATA 2022 (Rev. Mod. Phys. 97, 025002, 2025-04-30; NIST cuu, content May 2024) — the pinned constants release, with the seven exact defining constants (Δν_Cs, c, h, e, k, N_A, K_cd) and GUM uncertainties on measured constants (G, α, masses). Prior art to mine, not depend on: SageManifolds (SageMath 10.8) for frame/chart cost calibration; xAct+xPerm, Cadabra for abstract-index and spinor-representation typing; mathlib charted spaces (Gouëzel 2019) as the ceiling of chart rigor.

## avoid

Adopting any single unit standard verbatim: SI-verbatim loses affine/log/angle typing (its own footnote calls rad=m/m misleading → torque/energy and Hz/rad·s⁻¹ confusions type-check); UCUM-verbatim demotes mole to a dimensionless count (breaks the chemistry vertical) and discards annotations that carry entity semantics; QUDT-as-semantics cannot compute logarithmic units (DeciB conversionMultiplier = 0.0) → silent wrong conversions. Bare 'dB' or 'pH' without typed reference/base/convention → unsound 10-vs-20 and cross-reference arithmetic. A single Temperature type → 5 °C difference silently read as 278.15 K absolute. Hard-coded compiler constants → invisible staleness at the CODATA 2026 adjustment and a precision lie for ħ = h/2π. Entity counts as bare unit one with annotations → the documentation-enforced-convention failure Gate 1 names. Regime predicates over the model's own output (|pV/nRT−1| ≤ 0.02) → self-certifying models, false out_of_regime greens. Full manifold/chart machinery in v1 → mathlib/SageManifolds evidence says expert-years of cost with no vertical-slice payoff; conversely, frame-mixing without typed tags → frame errors become comments. Spinors as untyped column vectors, or claiming curved-space spinors without a declared tetrad → transformation law lives in documentation, a lie by the fidelity test.

## open_questions

- Dimension-vector exponents: integers (SI, UCUM) or small rationals? Amplitude spectral density (V/√Hz) is real laboratory vocabulary; admitting rationals changes the kernel type. Needs a corpus check of the vertical slice's actual quantities.
- Does angle-as-base-dimension round-trip large SI/ISO-conformant source corpora without an explosion of explicit erasure lifts (the historical reason SI keeps rad dimensionless)? Prototype against imported CODATA/IUPAC tables before freezing.
- How are CODATA inter-constant correlation coefficients stored and propagated through GUM uncertainty machinery — full covariance pack per release, or on-demand pairs? (CODATA publishes them; Stage A A.5.7 makes correlation first-class.)
- ISO 80000 parts 3/8 (space/time, acoustics) define the power vs root-power level quantities normatively — purchase and transcribe, or rely on secondary restatements? Currently evidence-incomplete.
- Which named Level operations ship (power_sum for incoherent dB addition, cascade_gain, pH mixing is NOT linear) — the operation whitelist is chemistry/acoustics domain content, not kernel.
- Does the mole/Count<E> bridge live in the physics IR or the chemistry IR? N_A is exact so the lift is lossless, but ownership determines which schema declares it.
- Renormalization-scheme context (value, μ, scheme, loop order) — is any v1–v2 vertical actually consuming running couplings, or is this schema-reserved-only? If reserved, mark unsupported rather than half-implementing.
- Interaction with the Stage A [open] invalid_model question: is a dimensional inhomogeneity in a declared equation invalid_model (disposition) or a compile-time invalid_input? The unit kernel makes it detectable at both points.
